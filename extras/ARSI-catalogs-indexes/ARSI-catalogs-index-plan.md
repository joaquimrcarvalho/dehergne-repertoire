## 3. Key finding — the typewritten indexes

Every major volume except **LUS. 43 II** carries a typewritten index (probably
made at ARSI) that lists, per year and per house, the folio where each catalogue
starts. One page can replace reading hundreds of images.

| Volume | Index location | Yield |
|---|---|---|
| LUS. 39 | `LUS.39.0.index.pdf`, p. 2 ("Index ad codicem ARSI Lus.39") | 23 catalogues 1579–1686, with dates, folios, supplements and remarks such as "pro 1614" |
| LUS. 44 I+II | `LUS. 44 I/000a.jpg` | 1587–1645, ff. 1–655; marks the LUS.44-I / LUS.44-II split |
| LUS. 45 | `000i1.jpg`, `000i2.jpg`, `000i3.jpg` | 1649, 1651, 1654/55, 1658, 1661, 1665, 1669, 1672, 1675 — Cat. I / II / III ranges per year |
| LUS. 46 | `000v.jpg` | 1678, 1681, 1685, 1690, 1693, 1696 |
| LUS. 47 | `000v.jpg` | 1700–1726 |
| **LUS. 43 II a/b** | **none found** (covers and ends checked) | use existing VLM tables + verification |
| LUS. 42, LUS. 40/41, LUS. 46a | no typed index identified | small volume / special series — inspect individually |

The filenames of the images are the **folio numbers** (e.g. `LUS. 45/251.jpg` =
f. 251). Confirmed by the stamped folio at the foot of the page for LUS. 45.
The correspondence must still be checked at the boundaries for LUS. 43, 46, 47.

### 3.1 Prior automated work does not replace the indexes

The notes `ARSI Lusitania Catalogues LUS. 39/43 II a/43 II b/44 I/44 II/45`
contain catalogue tables generated by tagging pages with a local vision model
(`StartCatalog` / `Verde` / `Green`). Problems:

- dates are frequently hallucinated (e.g. "1783", "1873", "1892", "1733");
- several catalogues are missed or split wrongly (see the reconciliation note);
- **the tagging script/pipeline is not on disk** — the search for
  `StartCatalog` only finds the resulting notes (it was an external QoderWork run).

Therefore: **typewritten indexes are the primary source**; the VLM tables are a
secondary source, useful mainly for LUS. 43 II.

## 4. Method (economical pipeline)

1. **Index extraction (per volume).** Locate the typed index (front/back/PDF),
   read 1–3 images, transcribe into a machine table:
   `ano | tipo | fólio inicial | fólio final | título/notas`.
2. **Reconciliation.** Compare that table with the master list and the notes on
   disk → list of missing / duplicate / misnamed catalogues.
3. **Note generation (no vision needed).** One note per catalogue, generated from
   the table, embedding the images by folio range in the existing style
   (`![251.jpg](file:///…/LUS.%2045/251.jpg)`), plus title and link to the volume note.
4. **Verification (1 image per catalogue).** Read only the **first folio** of each
   catalogue to confirm header/date; automatically check that every embedded file
   exists and that the stamped folio in the image matches the filename at range
   boundaries.
5. **Master-list update.** Replace bare year entries with sub-bullets; fix the
   3 dangling links; link the 27 orphan notes.
6. **QA.** Reconciliation report per volume: images ↔ catalogues, unaccounted
   folios, missing folios (e.g. LUS. 47 ff. 253–349).

Naming to adopt for new notes (per the "Instruções de registo" in the master list):

```
ANO-TIPO-LOCAL(se relevante)-LUS-VOLUME[-parte]-FÓLIO
e.g. 1658-T1-LUS-45-251, 1568-T6P-Coimbra-LUS-43-II-a-326
```

Old notes are **not renamed** (avoid breaking links); `c`-prefixed folios and
`LUS-43-II` vs `LUS-43-II-a` inconsistencies are kept and documented.

## 6. Feasibility

**Feasible.** Estimated remaining output:

- ~120–150 new or consolidated catalogue notes;
- ~10–15 index-page reads;
- ~120–200 one-page verifications;
- master-list cleanup (3 dangling links, 27 orphan notes, duplicates).

With 4–5 agents split by volume the task is a few hours; single-agent it is 2–3
days.

### Risks

- **LUS. 43 II a/b has no typed index** (537 images, 1540–1579). Must rely on the
  existing VLM tables, whose dates are unreliable → verify every catalogue start.
- **Filename ↔ stamped-folio offsets** must be checked at boundaries for
  LUS. 43, 46 and 47 (confirmed for LUS. 45). The continuation folder uses
  two-folio filenames (`0588v-0589.jpg`).
- **Missing folios**: LUS. 47 ff. 253–349 are absent from the digitalisation
  (recorded in `INDICE-folios-em-falta-Portugal-no-entry.md`); indexes must
  record gaps rather than invent entries.
- **Duplicates**: several catalogues exist in more than one volume/copy
  (e.g. 1569 Coimbra; 1654/1655 in LUS. 45); reconciliation must decide the
  canonical link.

## 7. Decisions pending

1. **Verification level** — verify the first folio of *every* catalogue
   (recommended) or trust the typed indexes and spot-check 20%.
2. **Granularity** — separate notes for T1 / T2 / T3 per year (recommended, as in
   the trial) or one note per year.
3. **LUS. 43** — accept slightly lower confidence (VLM + verification) or budget
   more page reads.
4. **Scope** — include the special volumes (LUS. 40/41 general assistancy,
   LUS. 42, LUS. 46a) in this pass or leave them out.
5. **Note location** — new notes go to `03 Projectos/` (as in the trial) or
   `01 Notes/`.

### Resultado

- **274 catálogos indexados e ligados a notas** em 12 volumes (290 registos nas tabelas,
  incluindo índices/suplementos documentados sem nota).
- **171 notas novas** criadas em `03 Projectos/`; **103 notas antigas reaproveitadas**
  (não duplicadas, não renomeadas).
- **Master list atualizada**: nova secção
  «Índice sistemático por volume (gerado 2026-10-22)» com 274 linhas (ano | tipo | fólios | nota),
  ponteiro no topo, links pendentes corrigidos.
- Tabelas: `data/*.json` (12) · Relatórios: `reports/LUS-*.md` (9) + `reports/SUMMARY.md`.
- `validate.py`: **0 problemas** (notas existentes, todos os links `file://` resolvem,
  sem intervalos sobrepostos).
- Nenhum ficheiro da digitalização foi movido, renomeado ou copiado.

### O que o ensaio não previa

- Em LUS. 39, LUS. 44 e LUS. 46 os **nomes dos ficheiros ≠ fólios carimbados** (desvios
  progressivos por folhas não filmadas). As notas embebem os ficheiros corretos; as tabelas
  guardam a foliação carimbada e a gama de imagens em separado.
- LUS. 40/40a/40b revelaram-se **três volumes distintos** (registo de 1691 + listas de
  expulsão; catálogos de 1741-1742; Status impresso de 1760 + catálogo de 1775).
- LUS. 41 é o **Catalogus generalis Assistenciae Lusitaniae de 1768** (só os ficheiros
  3–196 são catálogo; 197–911 são índice alfabético e cartas/relações, fora do âmbito).
- LUS. 46 a é o **Catálogo I de 1700**, variante do T1 de 1700 do LUS. 47 (duplicado parcial).
- LUS. 43 (sem índice dactilografado) ficou com **24 segmentos inferidos** por verificar.
