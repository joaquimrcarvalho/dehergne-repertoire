## Identification

**Conclusion.** **Not identified.** The place written in Dehergne's notice
no. 727 as *« Lao chan oua, Hou quang »* — where Nicolas-Marie Roy pronounced
his final vows on 15 August 1759 — occurs **once** in the printed literature;
the source Dehergne quotes (ARSI *Gallia* 24, f. 392) is not in this archive,
Pfister's notice of Roy gives the vow **date but never the place**, and Roy is
**not** among the six missionaries of the 茶园沟 / Kou-tch'eng station listed in
Pfister's index, so the tempting assignment to that station has no documentary
basis. The best **phonetic reading** of the romanisation is **老山洼
(Lǎoshānwā, "old-mountain hollow")** — an **inference only**: no source gives
characters, Wikidata holds no such item, and no 老山\* village is recorded in
Hubei. Full research note: `notes/lao-chan-oua-identification.md`.

Source status: `sources/dehergne-r.cli:1932` —
`ls$jesuita-votos-local/Lao chan oua, Hou-kouang/17590815` carries **no**
`@wikidata`; the interim option used elsewhere for a bare Hukwang stay is the
province-level anchor `Q2235155` (Qing Huguang) with "name unresolved", or
leaving the line bare.

*Page-number correction.* The sibling notes `chayuangou-identification` and
`chayuangou` cite Pfister's *Table alphabétique* s.v. *Kou-tch'eng* at printed
p. **1039**; the archive page is printed p. **1049** (doc 50, p. 515). The entry
and its six names (Labbe, de Neuvialle, Bataillé, Loppin, de la Roche, de
Lamathe) are as quoted there — and **Roy is absent from it**.
