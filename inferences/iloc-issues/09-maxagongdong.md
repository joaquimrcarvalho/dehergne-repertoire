## Identification

**Conclusion.** **Not identified.** The only attestation in this archive is the
closing line of Dehergne's notice no. 916 (Zárate, Nicolás [a], espagnol),
printed p. 303: *« En 1725 il était à Maxagongdong (Philipp. 4, 126v). »* The
register behind it — **ARSI *Philippinae* 4, f. 126v** (the year-1725 catalogue
of the Philippine province) — is **not held by this archive**, and the archive
contains **no 18th-century Philippine Jesuit catalogue**: its Philippine-facing
material (*Documentos para a História da Missão Portuguesa do Oriente*, vols 7,
11, 12) stops at 1582, and the *jesuit-catalogues* collection holds only
Portugal. The word is not shaped like Dehergne's French sinological
romanisation, and no Chinese reading is offered for it; a Spanish transcription
of a Philippine station name is the natural reading (inference), but nothing in
the archive or in any source consulted attests it. **No Wikidata id is
