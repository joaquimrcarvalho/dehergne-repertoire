## Identification

**Conclusion.** Identified: **« Pe-chieh » = Pé-kié / Pé-kia = 白家 (Dehergne:
"ou mieux" 白加)**, the Christian *bourg* of **Sinfeng = 信豐縣 Xinfeng hien,
贛州府 Kanchow (Ganzhou) fu, Kiangsi** → district anchor `Q1356667` (Xinfeng
County). Dehergne's cross-reference (*AHSI* 36 (1967), 230) is **correct as
printed** — it is his own Kiangsi gazetteer entry 14 « Sinfeng 信豐 (Sin-fong)
hien du Kanchow », whose next paragraph reads « **Adrien Greslon meurt le
3 mars 1696 à Pé-chieh du Sin-feng** », and which names the chrétienté
« **Chrétienté : 白家 ou mieux 白加 Pé-kié (en dialecte), Pé-kia** appelé aussi
Pé-kia-tsuen … Pé-kia-pao ». Pfister independently names « **le bourg de
"Pé-kia"** » among the chrétientés dependent on the Kan-chow residence (notice
104, printed pp. 295-296). That AHSI article is **not in the PHA archive**
(read on the Internet Archive scan); the characters were read on the page image
because the OCR of docs 65/73/150 drops CJK. The 新淦 / 新干 reading floated in
this file is *not* the place: 信豐 (Sin-**fong**) ≠ 新淦 (Sin-**kan**, 臨江府), and
Dehergne expressly distinguishes it from two other *Sinfeng* (non-*hien*) in
Kiangsu and Chekiang. The village itself has no Wikidata item, so the id belongs
on the district. Full research note:
`notes/pe-chieh-sinfeng-identification.md`.

**Village absent.** The bare `白家` query returns **50** village records across
China (the 1911 layer does carry Jiangxi villages — e.g. `hvd_140558` 白家集
under 进贤县), but **none has parent 信丰县 or 赣州府**; the parent-facet queries
(`白家`/`白家村`/`白家堡` with `--parent 信丰县`) return **0**. `白加` returns
**0** everywhere. TGAZ therefore does not attest the chrétienté itself.

**Parent units confirmed.** `hvd_838` — 信丰县 (Xinfeng Xian), county (縣),
CHGIS **1820** layer, parent **赣州府** (Ganzhou Fu); 25.3784 N, 114.93108 E;
`present_location` "江西信丰县". The long record `hvd_40841` (742–1911) traces
信豐縣 through 南康郡 → 虔州 → 赣州 → 赣州路 → **赣州府** (1365–1911), i.e. the
Kanchow-fu parent the issue requires. `hvd_32567` 赣州府 (府) spans 1365–1911.
Canonical record: <https://chgis.hudci.org/tgaz/placename/json/hvd_838>.

**Coordinates.** TGAZ county seat 25.3784, 114.93108 vs Wikidata `Q1356667`
(信丰县 Xinfeng County) 25.28806, 114.90167 → **10.5 km** (county seat vs
modern county centroid — normal).

**Verdict: `partially-confirmed`.** TGAZ confirms the county (信豐縣) and its
prefecture (赣州府 / Ganzhou), which is the whole administrative frame of the
issue's reading, but it has **no record of the 白家/白加 bourg itself** in its
1820/1911 village layers. The identification of « Pe-chieh » as 白家 in 信豐縣
rests on the archive evidence (Dehergne AHSI 1967, Pfister) and is neither
confirmed nor rejected at village level by TGAZ.
