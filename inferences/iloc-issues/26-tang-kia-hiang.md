## Identification

**Conclusion.** Partly identified. T'ang-kia-hiang is a **small Christian community
(chrétienté), not a town**, near **Songjiang 松江, Kiangnan**, on the **Huangpu** river,
where Laimbeckhoven withdrew to die on 22 May 1787. Pfister gives the Chinese form
**湯家巷** (t. 2, printed p. 769 — read from the page image, because the OCR of that page
drops all CJK), in the sentence "*se retira dans la petite chrétienté de T'ang-kia-hiang
湯家巷, non loin de Songkiang et des bords du Hoang-p'ou*". Two qualifications:
**(a)** the archive's two authorities disagree on the first character — Pfister prints
湯家巷 (Tāng 湯), while Dehergne's dialect form "*Daong-ka-hang*" has a voiced initial,
which points to 唐家巷 (Táng 唐, the form used in this file's title); the archive contains
no third witness, so neither should be treated as certain. **(b)** **No village-level
Wikidata item is justified**: there is no 湯家巷 item on Wikidata, and the two 唐家巷村
items are other places (`Q13992701` in 花園鎮, 岳池縣, Sichuan; `Q13756716` in Shaanxi).
The justified anchor is the Songjiang level — `Q11104671` (Songjiang Fu 松江府), which is
what the source `.cli` already carries, or `Q662380` (Songjiang 松江區) for the modern
district. Full research note: `notes/tang-kia-hiang-identification.md`.

Source note: `sources/dehergne-l.cli:328` already reads
`ls$morte/T'ang-kia-hiang#perto de Sungkiang @wikidata:Q11104671, Kiangnan/17870522#ou 17870520`,
and the database likewise holds `morte@wikidata = Q11104671 @ 17870522`, i.e. the coarse
Songjiang anchor recommended here is **already in the source** (the issue's `❗missing`
flag is a pipeline artefact, not a gap in the `.cli`). No village-level id exists to add.
