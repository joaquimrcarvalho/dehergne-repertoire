## 1. What the source says

**Dehergne, *Répertoire des Jésuites de Chine de 1552 à 1800* (1973), entry no. 727 — "Roy, Nicolas-Marie (franç.) P.", printed p. 233** (the vow line, verbatim):

> «V. **« Lao chan oua, Hou quang »**, 15 août 1759, pr. (Gal. 24, 392).»

The archival reference is the primary source: **ARSI, *Gallia* 24, f. 392** — the French province's vow register, **not consulted** here. Dehergne prints the place in guillemets, his device for a source spelling he could not match to a known place; he adds no characters, no gloss, no cross-reference. The rest of the entry: born Dijon 12 March 1726; entered Nancy 7 April 1743; ordained Paris 18 March 1752; embarked Lorient on the *Condé* 29 Dec. 1753 **with the Chinese Jesuits Lieou, Lan and Maur Tsao**; arrived Macao 15–16 Aug. 1754; **Nov. 1755, Hou-kouang** (SOCP t. 51, 1757, f° 536); died 8 Jan. 1769 "en barque « au milieu du Hou-quang »".

The name occurs **nowhere else in the Répertoire** (searched; the 1701 residence list and the chronology do not carry it).

## 2. Pfister's notice does not name the station — and his index excludes Roy from the mountain colony

**Pfister, *Notices*, t. 2, notice no. 407 — "Le P. Nicolas-Marie Roy", printed pp. 872–876**, built almost entirely from Roy's published letters:

- heading: *"N. 12 (?) mars 1726. — E. 7 avril 1743. — A. 15 août 1754. — **P. 15 août 1759. — M. 8 janv. 1769, au Hou-koang**"* — the vow date is given, **the place is not**;
- left Macao for his mission 16 June 1756, arrived at his post "la veille du jour de l'Assomption" 1756 after a month of travel; his apostolate was itinerant and boat-based: *"Nous les faisons [nos voyages] sur des barques…"*; in 1759, 150 baptisms and 1,300 confessions; *"Je suis placé… au milieu du Hou-koang"* (letter 117, to the bishop-count of Noyon);
- **Decisive negative**: Pfister's *Table alphabétique*, s.v. **Kou-tch'eng** (printed p. 1049) — the "Cévennes de la Chine" mountain colony — lists *"les PP. Labbe, 634; de Neuvialle, 724; Bataillé, 730; Loppin, 795; de la Roche, 800; de Lamathe, 883"*. **Roy is not in that list.** His vows therefore cannot simply be assigned to the colony's seat (茶园沟/磨盘山, resolved in #4/#62) on the strength of the district label.

## 3. Mission context — close to the mountain mission, but not located in it

- Roy embarked (1753) **with P. Maur Tsao**, who vowed at 茶园沟 (Tcha-yuen-keou) on 26 March 1759; Lamathe vowed at the same place 1 April 1759. Roy's own vows, 15 Aug. 1759 (Assumption), fall in the same mission and the same year — but at a **different, unnamed station**.
- At Roy's death (Jan. 1769) the priest who "accouru à la première nouvelle" to give the last sacraments was **P. de la Roche** (Pfister, p. 876) — one of the six Kou-tch'eng missionaries of the index. De la Roche's own notice (no. 362, printed p. 800) gives the mountain mission's timeline: he replaced Bataillé in the *montagnes de Kou-tch'eng 谷城* in 1743, fled the 1756 persecution, then resumed; he was still receiving vows in Hou-koang in 1771 (Léon Baron's profession, notice no. 424, printed p. 912 — the event behind issue #50). It does not mention Roy and names no station below the mountain range. Roy's circuit thus plainly adjoined the mountain mission's network, in the Han-river upper reaches of north-west Huguang; that is context, not a location.

## 4. The phonetic reading, and its negative check

In Dehergne's French-based romanisation **`chan`** renders *shan* 山 (as in *Chan-si* = Shansi) and a final **`oua`** renders *wa* — the syllable 洼 (*wā*, "hollow/depression"), a common hamlet suffix in the Han-jiang highlands. **老山洼 "Lao-shan-wa", "old-mountain hollow"** is therefore the best reading of `Lao chan oua`; alternatives (老屋湾, 老山寨) do not fit the phonetics.

**But no source gives characters, and the reading cannot be corroborated**: Wikidata holds **no 老山洼** item of any kind, and a SPARQL sweep of village items labelled 老山\* returns only namesakes in Shaanxi, Jilin, Gansu, Heilongjiang and Anhui — **none in Hubei**. With no characters, no gazetteer hit and no second attestation, proposing a modern item would be a guess, not an identification.

## 5. What remains open, and what would settle it

- **ARSI, *Gallia* 24, f. 392** — the vow register itself: a fuller form of the name (with characters?) may stand next to the formula Dehergne transcribed.
- **Roy's published letters** — *Lettres du Père Roy*, Lyon, Périsse frères, 1822 (2nd ed. 1824; 3rd ed. Lyon/Paris 1831 and Paris 1840 — the edition Pfister used; his bibliography, printed p. 877), and the Munich MS correspondence (*Catal. MSS*, t. VII, n° 1178); also *Lettres édifiantes*, éd. Panthéon, t. IV, pp. 79–95, 117, 124, 129, 139, 272, and **Chaney, *Le « Saint du Hou-koang »*, pp. 40–65** (cited by Pfister's heading). None was available to this check.
- **Dates that differ between the sources** — Backer-Sommervogel (t. 7, printed pp. 257–258) repeats Pfister's **Langres** birth, which Dehergne corrected to **Dijon** from the municipal archives (B 566 f 26); gives the novitiate as **Pont-à-Mousson** (the seat of the Nancy novitiate); and offers three death days — **5, 8 or 10 January 1769** — against Dehergne's and Pfister's 8th.
- **Source fix when resolved**: `sources/dehergne-r.cli:1932` — `ls$jesuita-votos-local/Lao chan oua, Hou-kouang/17590815` currently has **no `@wikidata`**; if the province-level anchor is wanted meanwhile, the pattern used for bare Hukwang stays (`@wikidata:Q2235155`) with `name unresolved` noted.
