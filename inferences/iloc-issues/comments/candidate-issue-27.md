## 1. What the source says

**Dehergne, *Répertoire des jésuites de Chine de 1552 à 1800* (1973), entry no. 830 — "Tch'en, Pierre (chin.)", pp. 265–266** (the vow formula on p. 266):

> «V. 8 sept. 1745, c. spir., à **Choui tcheou San Kiao**, chapelle de la B. Vierge Marie entre les mains du P. LEFEBURE. Il signe: Petrus CHIN, avec son nom chinois; de même, le P. LEFEBURE (Gal. 28, 191).»

i.e. vows as **coadjutor spiritualis**, 8 September 1745, in the **chapel of the Blessed Virgin Mary at Choui-tcheou San-kiao**, received by the French Jesuit **Louis-Joseph Le Febvre**; both signed with their Chinese names. Dehergne notes that *Franc. 21, 339* wrongly places the event at **Pékin** in 1745.

The man (same entry, pp. 265–266): a Chinese Jesuit, also written *Chin, Schin, Xin*, «**Petrus Sina**»; Chinese name **陳多祿 Tch'en To-lo** (*To-lo* = 多祿, "Petrus"); born 30 November 1688 at **Suzhou** (Soochow), Kiang-nan; a physician converted by a Christian friend; entered the Society **3 August 1734**; Hou-kouang, then **Kiangsi 1739**, Linkiang 1743, Wusih and Ningpo 1748; arrested at Suzhou in 1759; died **23 December 1768** in China.

**Note on the spelling.** The printed text reads **San Kiao** — 橋 = *kiao* — in both Dehergne entries concerned (nos. 830 and 459); "San Kia" is the form in the issue title and in the database field. This is the toponym 三橋, **not** the Buddhist–Confucian phrase 三教 *san-kiao* ("the three religions"), which has the same transcription.

## 2. Dehergne identifies the place himself

**Dehergne, "Les chrétientés de Chine de la période Ming (1581–1650)", *Monumenta Serica* 16 (1957), p. 70, note 11** (Kiangsi section):

> «JUICHOW (Joei-tcheou) **瑞州府** fou, actuel **Kaoan 高安**. M.: Xuicheu; A.: **Choui-tcheou fou**. Un certain 胡應茂 Hou In-meou émigre de **San-kiao 三橋** (bourg à **15 li sud-est de Juichow**) à Yangchow 揚州 du Kiangsu en 1638 […] mais il revient plus tard à San-kiao, baptisé avec sa famille.»

| Vow form / Dehergne spelling | Chinese | Identification |
|---|---|---|
| `Choui tcheou` (also `Jouei-tcheou`, `Juichow`, `Joei-tcheou`) | **瑞州府** | prefecture of Jiangxi; seat = present-day **高安 (Gao'an)** |
| `San Kiao` / `San-kiao` | **三橋** | market town **15 li** (≈ 8.6 km, Qing li ≈ 0.576 km) **south-east of the prefectural city** |

The article's place index repeats the cross-reference under the place: «**Sankiao** — Kgsi 1 n. 11» (1957, **p. 132**) — Kiangsi, section 1, note 11, the note quoted above. The vow place is therefore 瑞州三橋, "Sanqiao of Ruizhou".

## 3. The same chapel four years earlier (issue #55)

**Dehergne 1973, entry no. 459 — "Le Febure (Lefèvre; Le Febvre), Louis-Joseph (franç.)", p. 148**:

> «V. "**San kiao hou kia**" [près de Juichow (Jouei-tcheou), Kiangsi] 19 mars 1741, pr. (Gal. 22, 59). Il signe Ludov. Joseph LEFEBURE.»

Le Febvre — the very priest who received Tch'en's vows — had himself pronounced his final vows at the same place four years earlier. **Issue #55 (`San kiao hou kia`, Le Febvre, 1741-03-19) and this issue are the same locality**, and one identification resolves both. The fuller form *San-kiao hou-kia* reads naturally as **三橋胡家**, "Sanqiao of the Hu family", and fits the community named in §2: it was one **胡應茂 Hou In-meou**, of San-kiao, who moved to Yangzhou in 1638 and later returned with his household, baptised. (The characters 胡家 are an inference — Dehergne gives none for *hou kia*.)

## 4. Where it is today

- 瑞州府's seat, 高安, is now the county-level city **高安市 (Gao'an), 宜春市 Yichun, 江西省 Jiangxi**. Its modern urban core is **Q14583746 瑞州街道 (Ruizhou Subdistrict)** — note that this is a *subdistrict*, not the historical prefecture (see §5).
- **Wikidata Q14583988 — 三桥村 (Sanqiao village)**. Its chain: P131 → **Q14583980 荷岭镇 (Heling Town)** → **Q1229428 高安市** → Q363326 宜春市 → Q57052 江西省. Its administrative-division code (P442) `36 09 83 101 203` nests exactly through Heling (`36 09 83 101`) and Gao'an (`36 09 83`); the town item's own *contains* (P150) list includes Q14583988. **The village item has no coordinate (P625)**; the nearest item that has one is the town.
- **Direction and distance check.** From **瑞州街道 28.42811 N, 115.37494 E** (the old prefectural seat) to Heling Town 28.35111 N, 115.41594 E is **9.4 km on a bearing of 155° (SSE)**; from the 高安市 item's coordinate it is 9.0 km at 152°. Dehergne's **15 li south-east** ≈ **8.6 km** with the Qing li. The two agree to gazetteer rounding.
- **Caveats.** (i) The modern village is matched by name, administrative position and distance/direction — not by any local record of the 18th-century chapel; the chapel site itself is unlocated. (ii) Because Q14583988 has no coordinate, the measured distance is to the *town* centre; the village committee lies a little further out. (iii) Wikidata holds many items named 三桥村, most described only as "village in China"; this one is identified solely by its P131/P442 chain. (iv) For the historical framework use **Q15449420 瑞州府**; there is no Wikidata item for the historical San-kiao.

2. **`deh-louis-joseph-le-febure` links 瑞州府 to the wrong entity.** Its line
   `ls$jesuita-votos-local/San kiao hou kia#perto de Juichow (Jouei-tcheou) (pinyin Ruizhou 瑞州府 @wikidata:Q14583746), Kiangsi/17410319`
   points to **Q14583746 瑞州街道 (Ruizhou Subdistrict)**, a modern subdistrict of Gao'an, while the text says **瑞州府**. The prefecture is **Q15449420**. Either relabel as the subdistrict, or (better) use Q15449420 for 瑞州府 and add Q14583988 for the vow place itself.

## 6. A date discrepancy worth recording

**Pfister, *Notices biographiques et bibliographiques sur les jésuites de l'ancienne mission de Chine*, t. 2, notice no. 340 (Pierre Tch'en), pp. 738–739**, gives «Coadj. spir. **8 sept. 1740**» where Dehergne has the vows on **8 Sept. 1745**, and «M. après 1760» against Dehergne's 23 December 1768. The same notice records the "**Pater Petrus Sina**" whom Bahr, Gogeisl and Hallerstein met at "Xa-hu" in Kiangsi on 27 April 1739 — Dehergne's «Petrus Sina» — and Tch'en's companionship with Le Febvre in the persecution of 1746. Dehergne's «Pf. 738» is a *printed page* reference to this notice, not a notice number: Pfister's *Table alphabétique* (**p. 1094**) gives «Tch'en (Le P. Pierre) — "Chin", "Petrus Sina", n° 340, pp. 738, 739»; Le Febvre's own notice is no. 345, **p. 742**.
