## Archive research report (2026-10-14)

Works cited: **Dehergne, *Répertoire des Jésuites de Chine* (1973)**; **Pfister, *Notices*, t. 2**; **Dehergne, « Les chrétientés de Chine de la période Ming (1581–1650) », *Monumenta Serica* 16 (1957)**. Page numbers are those printed in the volumes; every quotation is checked against the printed page cited with it.

### 1. The itinerary, compact

Lisbonne 31 Aug 1713 (JS 134, 442) · E. 1 May 1728 (*Lus.* 48, 44) · emb. 13 Apr 1735 (W 1824; *Lus.* 48, 200) · Macao seminary 1737, theology, priest before 1737 (JS 134, 434) · named Court astronomer 12 Jan 1738 (JS 134, 435), arr. Beijing 1 May [1738] · Ho-kien-fou Christian community 1743–46 (Pf. 773–774) · vows 4V pr., Beijing 2 Feb 1747 (*Lus.* 16, 265) · 1749 Muran survey with Hallerstein (Pf. 774) · assessor of the Tribunal of Mathematics, mandarin 6th class 1753 (SOCP t. 50, 1755, f° 278; 1755 2nd order Pf. 774; 3rd class, tomb inscription) · vice-provincial 1753–59 · **Dzungaria surveys**: left Beijing 21 Mar 1756 (SOCP t. 51, 1757, f° 528) and 1759, with d'Espinha, 43 positions, north to Ili, rejoining at Sou-tcheou 肅州 Kan-sou (Pf. 774) · **Petit Tibet «Kinchuen» 20 Aug 1774 and March 1777** (see §2) · president of the Tribunal of Mathematics from 1774, succeeding Hallerstein (Amiot, letter of 28 Sept 1777) · died Beijing 22 May 1781 (Planchet, p. 177).

### 2. What the printed pages say

**(a) Dehergne, *Répertoire des Jésuites de Chine* (1973), notice no. 692, printed p. 223.** Verbatim:

> «cartes de Dzoungarie (Eleuthes), part de Pékin le 21 mars 1756 (SOCP t. 51, 1757, f° 528), et 1759; et du **Petit Tibet « Kinchuen » 1774 et 1777**; délivre GLEYO, M.E.P., au Szechwan; 1774, président du Tribunal des Mathématiques (lettre d'AMIOT, 28 sept. 1777). Voyages, RHM 8 (1931), 43.»

«Kinchuen» occurs **only here** in the whole *Répertoire*. The guillemets are Dehergne's own gloss: the "Petit Tibet" of this entry *is* the place he romanises *Kinchuen* — this narrows the broad 18th-c. French "petit Tibet" (which could denote various regions west of Sichuan) to one named locality.

**(b) Pfister, *Notices*, t. 2, notice no. 353 (LE P. FÉLIX DA ROCHA), printed pp. 773–777.** Printed p. 773 carries the head of the notice: *« N. 31 août 1713. — E. 1 mai 1728. — A. 1738. — P. 2 fév. 1747. — M. 22 mai 1781, Pékin. »*, the Ho-kien chrétienté, and the 1749 Muran survey; printed p. 774 describes the two mapping journeys; printed p. 775 the Sichuan vice-roi; printed p. 777 the works list. Key passages verbatim:

- **p. 774**: «Deux fois encore, le 20 août 1774 (Lettres édif., t. IV, p. 237) et en mars 1777 (de Murr, Journal, t. VI, p. 265), il fut envoyé **dans le petit Tibet, dont K'ien-long venait de faire la conquête**, pour en lever le plan. Ce fut dans cette dernière occasion qu'il usa de toute son influence auprès du **vice-roi du Se-tch'oan**, son ami, pour délivrer M. Glevo, des Missions-Étrangères, détenu en prison depuis neuf ans.»
- **p. 775**: «En arrivant à **Tch'eng-tou [成都]**, le 13 ou 14 (?) avril 1777, il demanda si M. Gleyo était délivré…» … «il revint à Pékin à la fin d'août, plus content d'avoir délivré un missionnaire et de pauvres néophytes, que d'avoir fait plaisir à l'empereur **en lui rapportant de très belles cartes de ses nouvelles conquêtes**.»
- **p. 777** (works list, item 5): «**Il a levé les Cartes du petit Tibet**, dont K'ien-long venait de faire la conquête (1774-1777). (Cf. ci-dessus, p. 774. Je n'en connais pas le titre et n'en ai vu aucune copie. L'ouvrage **衛藏圖識 Wei ts'ang t'ou che**, imprimé vers 1792 par deux officiers chinois, renferme peut-être les cartes du P. da Rocha. C'est un itinéraire au Tibet avec cartes. (Cf. Wylie, Notes, p. 52.)» — Pfister prints the title romanised as *Wei ts'ang t'ou che*; the characters **衛藏圖識 are supplied from that romanisation** (inference, labelled; the work is the known 1792 itinerary to Ü-Tsang).

Pfister prints **no Chinese characters and no second spelling** for the place — «le petit Tibet» only, three times (pp. 774 ×2, 777 ×1). The 1774 trip fell *during* the second campaign; «venait de faire la conquête» strictly fits the 1777 trip (campaign concluded 1776).

**(c) Negative checks.** Dehergne 1957 (Ming communities 1581–1650): no attestation — out of its period. No other occurrence of «Kinchuen», «Kin-tchouen», «Kin-chuen», «Kinchouen», 金川 is found in the consulted printed works. Backer-Sommervogel: no entry located for Félix da Rocha (consistent with Pfister p. 777 — the maps were never published under his name).

### 4. VERDICT — 🟢 confident, as a **region-level anchor**

**Petit Tibet «Kinchuen» = 金川 (Jinchuan)**, the Gyalrong Tibetan region of Qianlong's two campaigns (1747–49, 1771–76) on the Sichuan frontier, today **Aba (Ngawa) Tibetan and Qiang Autonomous Prefecture, Sichuan**. **Q338033 — 金川县 Jinchuan County** is the right anchor: the modern county occupies the **Greater Jinchuan (大金川) valley**, i.e. the territory of the Chuchen chiefdom (the county was actually named 大金川县 1953–59; Wikidata alias "Dajinchuan", Tibetan name Quqên). The candidate code in the issue is correct; it is verified on Wikidata as: P31 county; P131 → Q1012450 阿坝藏族羌族自治州 (whose P150 contains Q338033 — two-way check passes) and Q9574194 四川省 "Szechwan"; P625 31.478 N, 102.062 E; P442 `51 32 26`.

Reasoning chain, each step labelled:

1. **The author's own equation** (Dehergne 1973, printed p. 223): his «Kinchuen» glosses this person's "Petit Tibet".
2. **Kinchuen = Kin-ch'ouen = 金川** — *inference from romanisation* (both authors write the aspirated ch' as ch-: Dehergne's *Tcheou* 州, Pfister's *Se-tch'oan*). The context excludes homophones: no other "Kin-ch'ouan" region on the Sichuan frontier was conquered by Qianlong.
3. **Context match** (Pfister t. 2, printed pp. 774–775): a conquest «venait de faire» by 1774–77, reached through the **Sichuan vice-roi at Chengdu** (13–14 Apr 1777), maps of «ses nouvelles conquêtes» — this is the second Jinchuan war (1771–76), concluded 1776. Distance/direction check: Chengdu → Q338033 seat = **211 km at 296° (WNW)**, up the Dadu river — consistent; *no li distance is stated in any source consulted*, so this check is qualitative only.
4. **Anchor grade**: region-level, exactly like this person's own Dzungaria (Q284027) and Szechwan (Q19770) anchors. Labelled caveats: (a) the campaign region covered **both** 大金川 (modern 金川县 Q338033) **and** 小金川 (modern 小金县 Q1169539, ~60 km E of the Jinchuan seat) — no source says which valleys da Rocha surveyed on each trip; (b) no Wikidata item exists for the historical Jinchuan region/chiefdoms themselves, so the modern county is the best available anchor, and it covers the Greater-Jinchuan half.

### 5. What would settle it

- **The two primary letters**, unconsulted: *Lettres édifiantes et curieuses*, éd. Panthéon, t. IV, p. 237 (letter of 20 Aug 1774, likely Amiot's) and de Murr, *Journal*, t. VI, p. 265 (March 1777) — they may print the surveyed localities (e.g. the Greater-Jinchuan strongholds) and a fuller spelling. Also Amiot's letter of 28 Sept 1777 (Dehergne's citation for the presidency).
- **The maps' afterlife**, per Pfister's own leads (printed p. 777): Wylie, *Notes*, p. 52 and 衛藏圖識 (1792) — a study of their Jinchuan sheets could name the places.
- **Fix in the sources** (issue #51 proper): both `.cli` lines lack the anchor — suggest `ls$estadia/Petit Tibet «Kinchuen»#金川, região (Grande Jinchuan, nível região) @wikidata:Q338033/1774` and the same with `/1777` (`sources/dehergne-r.cli`, lines 806–807).
- **Loose ends met on the way** (record, out of scope here): `ls$titulo/Mandarim de 2ª Classe` is dated **1655** — Pfister printed p. 774 says **1755** (plain typo); `ls$nascimento` carries **1716**-08-31 — both Pfister (printed p. 773) and Dehergne (printed p. 223) say 31 **août 1713**; `ls$chegada/Pequim/17530501` conflates the 1753 assessor nomination with the 1738 Beijing arrival («arr. à Pékin le 1er mai» = 1 May 1738); `ls$estadia/Pequim/17560321` encodes «part de Pékin le 21 mars 1756» — a *departure* for the Dzungaria survey, not a stay.
