## Archive research report (2026-10-14)

Person: **Etienne Yang (Laforest)** (`deh-etienne-yang`; Chinese name per Dehergne: *Yang Tche-Tô Tô-wang*, Pfister: *Yang Té-wang* 楊德望). Event under study: `estadia` (arrest in the 1787 Kiangsi persecution), 1787-05. Reference works: Dehergne, *Répertoire des Jésuites de Chine* (1973), notice no. 911, p. 301; Pfister, *Notices*, t. 2, notice no. 427, pp. 920–922.

### 1. Itinerary (compact)

N. near Beijing 1733-02-08 · leaves Beijing for La Flèche and Paris 1751-07-07 (with Aloys Kao) · E. (novitiate) Paris 1759-03-10 · deacon Paris 1762-12-18, priest Paris 1763-05-28 (Card. de Beaumont, incardinated in Paris) · embarks Lorient 1765-02-01 on the *Duc de Choiseul* · Macao 1765-07-02 · Canton 1765-07-29 (leaves c. 17 Nov) · Beijing 1766-02-02 · **Kiangsi from 1767** · procureur of the French mission at Canton 1776-11-30 (charge transferred to Raux 1784-01-25) · **back in Kiangsi 1785; arrested at "Pé-pou, Linkiang fou", May 1787** · Beijing and Tartary 1789 · offers to go to Korea 1790 · M. 1798?, in Kiangsi?

### 2. What the printed sources say

- **Dehergne, *Répertoire des Jésuites de Chine* (1973), notice 911, p. 301.** The line, verbatim: *« 1785, de nouveau au Kiangsi (M 447, 1385) où arrêté à **Pé-pou du Linkiang fou**, mai 1787; en 1789, à Pékin et en Tartarie (M 448, 93), missions. »* The siglum is resolved by the book's own list (printed p. XXXIV, "III. Abréviations et sigles"): *« M. (MEP., AMEP.) Archives des Missions Etrangères de Paris (*) »* — so the primary source behind the place is **AMEP, vol. 447, p. 1385, not consulted**. "Pé-pou" occurs **nowhere else** in Dehergne 1973, Pfister or the 1957 gazetteer; **no printed source gives Chinese characters** for it.
- **Dehergne, *Les Chrétientés de Chine de la période Ming (1581–1650)*, *Monumenta Serica* 16 (1957), Kiangsi §II, note 4, printed p. 73:** *« **LINKIANG (Lin-kiang) 臨江 fou**, actuel **Tsingkiang (Ts'ing-kiang) 清江**, au sud de Nanchang. »* (the characters are as printed in the article). Ricci passed through in 1596 and gave its latitude; in 1650 Fr. de Gouvea had a church there (SF II 397). The same article's place index, **printed p. 130**, reads *« Linkiang, Lin-kiang — Kgsi 4 »*. This is the author's own identification of Linkiang fou: **臨江府 Linjiang fu**, Jiangxi, whose 1950s name was 清江 Qingjiang.
- **Dehergne, *Répertoire* (1973), notice 330 (Foucquet, Jean-François), printed p. 99:** *« 1703, **Linkiang fou**, Kiangsi; Nanchang, Fuchow fou et **Linkiang fou**, 1709 »* — the same prefecture, no station detail.
- **Dehergne, *Répertoire* (1973), notice 830 (Pierre Tch'en), printed p. 266:** *« 1743, **Linkiang**, Kiangsi (M 434, 713) »* — again the prefecture level only, and again from the MEP archives (not consulted).
- **Pfister, *Notices*, t. 2, notice 427 (LE P. ÉTIENNE YANG 楊德望), printed pp. 920–922.** Pfister tells the same arrest **without any place**: *« Ayant été trahi et dénoncé au mandarin par un mauvais chrétien, il fut **arrêté au commencement de mai 1787**, et mis en prison »* (printed p. 922, citing a letter of M. Dufresse, Macao, 8 Feb 1788, *Nouv. lettres édifiantes*, t. V, pp. 432–433 — not consulted). His footnote on Yang's Kiangsi residence (same printed page; Benoist letter of 10 Nov 1767: an *entrepôt* between Beijing and Canton) also names no station. **"Pé-pou" is Dehergne's addition, drawn from AMEP 447, 1385.**

### 4. VERDICT — not identified (and the fielded candidate Q13802007 is wrong)

- **The prefecture is settled; the station is not.** Linkiang fou = **臨江府 Linjiang fu**, Jiangxi — Dehergne's own characters (1957, printed p. 73). The Qing prefecture's seat is today **临江镇 Linjiang town, 樟树市 Zhangshu (county-level city, 宜春市 Yichun, Jiangxi)**; Dehergne's "actuel Tsingkiang 清江" is the pre-1988 name of Zhangshu. The event "happened somewhere in Linjiang fu" can be anchored to that area.
- **Q13802007 fails the geographic test and must not be used.** On Wikidata its administrative code (P442) is `32 03 82 103 213` — `32` = **Jiangsu**, not Jiangxi: the chain is 白埠村 → 官湖镇 → **邳州市 Pizhou** (`32 03 82`) → 徐州市 Xuzhou, Jiangsu. Measured from the old prefectural seat (Linjiang town, 28.005 N 115.398 E), that is 756 km at 18° (NNE) — another province entirely. Its English description "village in Jiangxi province, China" is **false** (a Wikidata description error), which is presumably what misled the attribution.
- **No Wikidata 白埠 item lies in the old Linjiang fu area** (its Qing counties = today's Zhangshu 樟树, Xingan 新干, Xinyu 新余, Xiajiang 峡江). The other existing 白埠 items are all elsewhere: Q14572317 (筠门岭镇, 会昌县, Ganzhou, southern Jiangxi, 309 km at 174° from the seat), Q14798450 (Fei county, Linyi, Shandong), Q13840268 (Sanmenxia area, Henan), Q14703940 白埠镇 (Pingdu, Shandong).
- **Phonetics (inference, labelled).** "pou" matches 埠 *bù* well (in the same line "fou" = 府 *fǔ*). But "Pé" is ambiguous: in Dehergne's transcription Pé = the *bei/po* syllable (Pékin 北京, Pé-t'ang 北堂 — the latter in his own abbreviation list), while he writes Mandarin 白 *bái* as "Pai"; 白 could still surface as "Pé" via a Gan-dialect checked syllable. So **白埠 is one plausible reading among several — 北埠 or 陂埠 fit the romanisation just as well** — and no source gives characters. A plausible-looking guess is worse than "not identified".
- Practical consequence for the project: field nothing for the station for now; if a link must be added to `ls$estadia/Pé-pou, Linkiang fou/17870500#preso` (dehergne-y.cli, line 30 — currently without `@wikidata:`), it should be the prefecture/seat area (临江镇 Q10877120 / 樟树市 Q197394, or the historical Q15449446 臨江府 — not to be confused with Q28414898 临江府, the Jilin prefecture of the same name), with the station itself marked unresolved.

### 5. What would settle it

1. **AMEP, vol. 447, p. 1385** — the document Dehergne cites; it may print the Chinese characters or a fuller locality for the arrest, and would also settle whether "Pé-pou" is his contraction.
2. The 1784–1787 Kiangsi persecution papers: Dufresse's letter of 8 Feb 1788 (*Nouv. lettres édifiantes*, t. V, pp. 432–433) and the other reports Dehergne mined (M 448, 93; RBS 396).
3. A Qing 《臨江府志》 or a modern place-name gazetteer of 樟树市/新干市/新余市/峡江县, looking for a 白埠 / 北埠 / 陂埠 village (or market-station 埠) within the old prefecture — then create/link the Wikidata item with its P131 chain and coordinate.
