## Archive research report (2026-10-14)

**Place**: "Yuwei" (source spelling « Juguei ») = **右卫 Youwei**, the former
garrison city of **右玉 Youyu** in northern Shanxi — today **右卫镇 (Youwei
Town), 右玉县 (Youyu County), 朔州市 (Shuozhou), 山西省 (Shanxi)**. Wikidata:
**[Q10916242](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q10916242)** (右卫镇; P31 town
Q735428, P442 `14 06 23 101`, P131 → [Q1196894](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1196894)
右玉县 — link verified both ways, the county's P150 contains the town; P625
40.16279 N, 112.35023 E). The county-level alternative is **Q1196894** (右玉县,
P442 `14 06 23`). Qing context: the garrison city was the seat of 右玉县 in
**朔平府** (Shuoping prefecture, est. 1725) — historical items
[Q113491095](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q113491095) 右玉县 (Qing) →
[Q10326808](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q10326808) 朔平府. Wikidata read
2026-10-14. **Do not use Q56533878 ("右卫")**: that item is the Chinese *imperial
guard institution* (zhwiki: 中国古代禁卫军指挥机构), not the place.

### 1. The person's itinerary (Dehergne 1973 no. 722, printed pp. 231–232; Pfister t. 1, notice 164, printed p. 410)

Born Macao 1667 of Chinese parents; entered the Society at Macao 28 Sept 1686;
arrived in the mission 1689 (16 Jan 1691, with P. Nogueira); Nanking; then
**Kiangchow = 绛州 Jiangzhou** (with P. Provana; Pfister adds 太原府 Tai-yuan
fu) in Shansi 1692, 1695; ordained at Canton March 1691 by Mgr Della Chiesa;
vows (c. spir.) 1 Nov 1700 at "Kiam cheu" = 绛州; **12 Feb 1718, Pékin — he
helps the famille Sounou (苏努 Suonu)** (Pf. 410); Tartarie; March 1725 Canton,
exile (took the name *Fang* Nov 1725 to re-enter the mission, JS 180, 143);
**1727 « Juguei » = Yuwei, Shansi**; 1729 Leaotung; 1730 Pékin (ill); 1731
Honan, then Kiangchow June–Aug; died Pékin 11 May 1736. Chinese name per
Pfister (read on the printed page): 何天章起文 Ho T'ien-tchang K'i-wen.

### 2. The printed attestations

- **Dehergne, *Répertoire des Jésuites de Chine* (1973), entry no. 722, printed p. 231.** The line, verbatim:
  *« 1727, « Juguei » (BN: O² n. 441 p. 16), **Yuwei du Shansi**; 1729,
  Leaotung; 1730, Pékin, malade; 1731, Honan, puis Kiangchow, juin-août 1731. »*
  — i.e. « Juguei » is the spelling of a **Bibliothèque Nationale (Paris)
  manuscript (O² 441, p. 16 — as cited, not consulted)**; "Yuwei du Shansi" is
  Dehergne's own normalisation. Same notice, same page: *« 12 févr. 1718, Pékin, il aide
  la famille Sounou (Pf. 410) »*; the notice closes on printed p. 232 with his death
  at Pékin, 11 May 1736.
- **Pfister, *Notices*, t. 1, notice 164 (Le P. François-Xavier a Rosario), printed p. 410:**
  *« En 1718, il en fut chassé par le vice-roi, et contraint de revenir à Pékin. **De là, il faisait des
  excursions dans les provinces voisines et jusqu'aux frontières de Tartarie
  pour y visiter les princes exilés par Yong-tcheng** [雍正] et leur porter
  les secours des Pères de Pékin et les consolations de la religion (1727).
  (Lettres édif., t. III, p. 447.) »* — Pfister names the visited princes but
  not their exile place, placing it "at the borders of Tartarie".
- **Pfister t. 1, notice 161 (Le P. Joseph Suarez), printed p. 400**: Suarez
  converted the famille **Sourniama (Sounou / Suonu)** from 1719 and
  fortified them *« surtout pendant leur exil en Tartarie »* (Lettres édif.,
  t. III, p. 391). **Notice 165 (Le P. Pierre-Thomas da Cruz), printed p. 411**: *« En
  1728, il visita les princes exilés en Tartarie »* (Lettres édif., t. III,
  p. 455). **Notice 233 (Parrenin), printed p. 509**: Parrenin's letters
  recount *« les longues souffrances »* of the Sourniama family under
  Yongzheng. **Notice 248 (Mourao), printed pp. 560–561**: the family helped the exiled Mourao; the exiles
  converted people and built churches "en Tartarie" (Welt-Bott 206).
  **T. 2, notice 311 (le P. Houo), printed p. 665**: a Chinese Jesuit
  (d. 1763; a different man from Rosario) who could visit the princes *« dans les
  lieux de leur exil »* more easily *« parce qu'il était chinois »*, baptised
  Sourniama's 13th son Stanislas end-1725 and the princess Rosalie in 1736.
  **T. 2, *Table alphabétique* s.v. Sourniama (printed p. 1089)**: family
  *« Sourniama, Sounou, branche de la famille impériale convertie: les PP. Jos. Suares, 400, 401; — Parrenin, 509, 510, 514, 515; — A. Pereira, 653 »*.
- **Dehergne, « Les chrétientés de Chine de la période Ming (1581–1650) », *Monumenta Serica* 16 (1957)**:
  Ming-period only — no 1727 stations; no "Yuwei" anywhere in the other printed works consulted.
- **External check (web, 2026-10-14)**: Qing sources and modern Chinese
  scholarship place the Yongzheng-era exile of the Suonu (苏努) family at
  **山西右卫 (Youwei, Shanxi)** — e.g. the Taiwan thesis 《蘇努家族與天主教信
  仰之研究》 abstract: *「致使蘇努家族遭流放山西右衛並被拿掉黃帶子」*
  (ndltd.ncl.edu.tw/handle/q5j4q8; CAPTCHA-gated on direct access, abstract
  read via search); Palace Museum article 《清宗室苏努举家信奉天主教》
  (dpm.org.cn/court/talk/204938.html, PDF-viewer-gated); zh.wikipedia 苏努.

### 4. VERDICT: 🟢 confident — **Yuwei = 右卫 (Youwei), modern 右卫镇, 右玉县, 朔州市, 山西省; Wikidata Q10916242** (county-level alternative Q1196894)

Reasoning:

1. **Phonetics.** Dehergne's own gloss "Yuwei" reproduces the pinyin of 右卫
   (Yòuwèi) exactly. The manuscript spelling « Juguei » parses as
   Portuguese-written *Iu-guei* = Yòuwèi: initial *j* used as semi-vowel /j/
   (older Portuguese orthography j/i), and *guei* for -uei/wèi with the
   prosthetic *g* before a w-glide — a Portuguese scribal habit. *(This
   phonetic parsing is an inference, labelled as such; Dehergne reached the
   same reading.)*
2. **Context — the decisive argument.** The notice itself says he "helped the
   famille Sounou" in Pékin (1718); Pfister says that from Pékin he made
   excursions "up to the borders of Tartarie" to visit **the exiled princes**
   (1727), citing *Lettres édifiantes* t. III, p. 447 (printed p. 410). The Sounou (Suonu 苏努)
   family — the imperial-clan converts at the centre of the Yongzheng
   persecution — were exiled in 1724–1726 precisely to **山西右卫 (Youwei,
   Shanxi)**, as attested by Qing sources and modern scholarship. The 1727
   "Yuwei" stay is therefore his documented visit to the exiled Sourniama
   princes at their place of exile.
3. **Geography.** 右卫/右玉 sits on the Shanxi–Inner Mongolia frontier —
   Pfister's "frontières de Tartarie" fits exactly; it is in Shanxi
   ("du Shansi" ✓), 347 km WNW of Pékin (the base of the excursions), 81 km W
   of Datong. No distance is stated in any source checked, so no
   distance/direction test beyond this consistency check was possible.
4. **Competing candidates dismissed.** 蔚州/蔚县 (Yu prefecture) would be
   romanised "Yu-tcheou/-chow" in Dehergne's system (cf. "Kiam cheu", "Su
   cheu"), never "-wei", and was not the Sourniama exile place; 榆次 Yuci has
   a second syllable ts'ǔ, not wèi. Neither matches « Juguei ». The same
   reasoning excludes **Q56533878 ("右卫" = the imperial guard institution)** and
   any id for 榆次/蔚县: the anchor must be the *place* 右卫, i.e. Q10916242
   (右卫镇) or its parent county Q1196894.

*Caveat, stated honestly*: no printed work consulted prints the characters 右卫
for this specific event — the identification rests on Dehergne's own
romanisation plus the Sounou exile documentation, which is convergent but
external to the *Répertoire*.

### 5. What would settle it

1. **BN Paris, ms. O² 441, p. 16** — the manuscript behind « Juguei »:
   its context (the Sounou visit) should settle the spelling and possibly the
   characters.
2. **Lettres édifiantes et curieuses, t. III, pp. 391, 447–455, 467** (ed.
   princeps or Mérigot) — Parrenin's and the visitors' letters on the
   Sourniama exiles; the place will appear there, likely with characters.
3. **Margiotti, *Il cattolicesimo nello Shansi dalle origini al 1738*** (Roma
   1958) — cited by Dehergne for this man ("MARGIOTTI, 173, 182-185"); the
   standard study of the Shansi stations including the Youwei exiles.
4. ARSI **JS 134, 422d** and **JS 180, 143** (the 1725 exile/return under the
   name Fang), as cited by Dehergne — not consulted.

Field note: the transcription's `Yuwei do Shansi` (Portuguese *do*) renders
the printed *« Yuwei du Shansi »*; when the `@wikidata:` tag is added, the
place line should carry **Q10916242** (the town that contains the old garrison
seat), or **Q1196894** if the county-level entity is preferred for mapping.
