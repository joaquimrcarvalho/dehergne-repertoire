## Archive research report (2026-10-14)

> Verdict: **🟡 district-level anchor confirmed — the specific locality not identified.** The death place is **Yen-p'ing 延平** (today **延平区 Yanping District, Nanping 南平, Fujian = Q1337959**, P131 → Q68534 verified). The Wikidata code fielded in the issue is **right at district level and should be kept at that level**: it is 延平区, not a village. The **盐山 "Yanshan" reading of "Yen-sam" has no support and should be dropped** — no source gives characters for Yen-sam. Bonus correction: the **"monte chamado da Cruz" is not at Yanping at all** — it is the burial hill **十字山 (Shizi Shan, "Cross Mountain") outside the North Gate of Fuzhou**, to which Aleni's body was transferred; Dehergne's Portuguese quotation places his death *at Yen-sam*, and the Cross hill belongs with the Fuzhou burial.

### The person (Dehergne 1973, entry no. 17, printed pp. 6–7)

Giulio Aleni (艾儒略 *Ngai Jou-lio Sseu-Ki*), Italian, b. Brescia 1582; entered Novellara 1600; priest ~1608; embarked 1609; Macao 1610; mission founder: **Shansi 1620**, **Fukien April 1625**; exiled to Macao 1639; **vice-provincial of China 1641, resident at Yenping during the Tartar conquest (1648)**. Itinerary highlights (from the project DB): Yangchow, Nankin, Shanghai, Hangchow (1619), Kiangchow/Shansi 1620, Fukien 1625-, Yenping 1641/1648, death 1649.

### The printed attestations

1. **The death line, verbatim** (Dehergne 1973, entry 17, printed p. 7): *«M. 10 juin 1649, à Yen-sam « para o monte qua chamao da Cruz », près du mont appelé de la Croix (**JA 49-V-13, 483v**), Yenping, Fukien (HS 47, 72, suivi par dit: en août 1649).»* — the primary source is **ARSI *Japonica* 49-V, f. 483v, in Portuguese** (not consulted). Dehergne transcribes "Yen-sam" from it; he gives no characters, no gloss.
2. **Dehergne, « Les chrétientés de Chine de la période Ming (1581–1650) », *Monumenta Serica* 16 (1957), §31, printed p. 40:** *« 31. YENPING (Yen-p'ing) 延平 fou, au centre de la province, actuel Nanping 南平. … Aleni, réfugié en 1647 meurt là le 3 août 1649, sous le supériorat de Diaz (Pf. 108, 126, 130, 131, 220; D. 291, 305; erreur de la vie chinoise d'Aleni, qui le fait mourir à Yentsin du Honan, Pf. 136). »* (the characters are as printed in the article). The same article's place index (printed p. 136) lists « Yenping, Fk 31, 40 » — the only "Yen-" Fukien heading is Yenping/延平.
3. **Pfister, *Notices*, t. 1, notice no. 30 (*Le P. Jules Aleni*), heading printed p. 126:** *"M. 3 août 1649, Yen-p'ing."* — **no more specific station**. The death narrative, printed **p. 131**: *"… alla recevoir au ciel la récompense de ses travaux, au mois d'août 1649, dans la ville de **Yen-p'ing 延平**; son corps fut transféré à **Fou-tcheou fou** et inhumé en dehors de la porte du Nord, sur la «Montagne de la Croix», **Che-tse-chan 十字山**."*

### Assessment

- **District anchor**: Yen-p'ing 延平 = the Qing county/prefecture seat on the Min river = today **延平区 (Yanping District), Nanping, Fujian** → `Q1337959` (P31 district; P131 → Q68534 Nanping; P442 `35 07 02`; P625 26.6396, 118.1718). The fielded candidate is **correct at district level and is the id to keep**; Q68534 (南平市 Nanping) is the coarser, prefecture-level alternative.
- **"Yen-sam"**: a Portuguese-register spelling (like the same entry's Portuguese quotation) of a locality at/near Yen-p'ing where Aleni actually expired. Possible readings (沿山? 岩山? 延山?) are **pure inference — no characters in any printed source consulted**; 盐山 is excluded (no phonetic or geographic support, and the issue's own ？ already doubted it).
- **Date discrepancy** (worth keeping): Pfister and HS 47 give **August 1649** (Pfister: 3 August); Dehergne 1957 also gives 3 August 1649; Dehergne 1973's primary register gives **10 June 1649**. The register (ARSI JA 49-V-13, f. 483v) would settle it.
- **Source fix**: `sources/dehergne-a.cli:421` — `ls$morte/Yean-sam, perto do monte chamado da Cruz, Yenping, Fukien/16490610` — recommended: anchor the *morte* to `@wikidata:Q1337959` with "Yen-sam name unresolved", and correct the transcription to keep Dehergne's exact form `Yen-sam` (the .cli has `Yean-sam`); the phrase "perto do monte chamado da Cruz" belongs with the Fuzhou burial hill (十字山), not with a Yanping hill.

### What would settle it

- **ARSI, *Japonica* 49-V, f. 483v** (the Portuguese account, not consulted) — the fullest form of the name;
- the early lives cited by Pfister (Couplet, *Catal.* 23; the Chinese lives, BN ms. chinois 1016–1017);
- a 延平区/南平市 gazetteer search for a 沿山/岩山-type toponym on the Yanping→Fuzhou (Min river) route.
