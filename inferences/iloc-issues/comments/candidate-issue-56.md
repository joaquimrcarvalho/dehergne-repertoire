## Archive research report (2026-10-14)

Person: **Cyr Contancin** (`deh-cyr-contancin`, Dehergne 1973 no. 192; Pfister t. 2, notice 256; Chinese name per Pfister: *Kong Tang-Sin Tong-P'ing*). Event under study: `jesuita-votos-local` (profession, "pr."), 1706-01-14.

### 1. Itinerary (compact)

N. Issoudun 1670-05-25 (baptised there, St-Cyr) · E. Paris 1688-09-09 · priest 1700 · embarkation on the *Amphitrite*, arrived Kwangchowwan 1701-11-15 (to overwinter) · **vows/profession at "Shaohing" 1706-01-14** · Chekiang (Tchö-kiang) 1707 · Pékin 1711, superior of the French residence 1716-1718 · leaves for Shansi 1718-10-20 · Kiangsi 1720, Nanchang 1721 · exiled to Canton 1725 · back in France 1731 (revises Du Halde's *Description de la Chine*) · sails again from Lorient 1732-11-10 · M. at sea near Cadiz 1732-11-21.

### 2. The attestations, with printed pages

- **Dehergne, *Répertoire des Jésuites de Chine* (1973), entry 192, printed pp. 59–60** (the notice opens at the foot of p. 59 and closes on p. 60): *« V. « Chao-him prov. Tche kiam »=Shaohing, 14 janv. 1706, pr. (Gal. 16, 108) »*. Two things follow from the printed page: (a) the sigil is **Gal.** — not "Gai." as misread into the project's `data.json`; per the book's own sigle list (printed p. XXXIV): *« Gal. Gallia, Jésuites de l'Assistance de France, section des ARSI »* — so the primary reference is **ARSI, *Gallia* 16, f. 108** (profession register; not consulted); (b) the author himself normalises the vow formula's « Chao-him prov. Tche kiam » to **Shaohing**. The entry also cites *Pf. 571*.
- **Pfister, *Notices*, t. 2, notice 256, printed pp. 571–573:** Pfister is **silent on the vows and on the first station** — *« Nous ne savons pas à quelle date le P. Contancin en débarqua, ni dans quelle province il fut missionnaire au commencement de son séjour en Chine. En 1707, il était au Tchékiang »* (printed p. 572). Dehergne's *Gal.* 16, 108 is thus the only source for the vow place; Pfister corroborates only the Chekiang period.
- **Dehergne, *Répertoire* (1973), entry 431 (K'ieou, Pierre, chin.), printed p. 135:** *« N. Shaohing (Chao-hing fou, Tchô-kiang), 1 mai 1686 (JS 134, 432). … Pf. 738 »* — the author's own gloss that Pfister's « Chao-hing fou » = **Shaohing**, Tchö-kiang.
- **Pfister, *Notices*, t. 2, notice 339 (Fr. Pierre K'ieou), printed p. 738:** *« Il naquit à Chao-hing 紹興府 au Tché-kiang, le 1er mai 1686 »* — **the characters 紹興府 are printed in Pfister** for exactly this romanisation, in this province.
- **Dehergne, « Les chrétientés de Chine de la période Ming (1581–1650) », *Monumenta Serica* 16 (1957), Chekiang §VII, entry 18, printed p. 20:** *« SHAOHING (Chao-hing) 紹興 fou, entre Hangchow et Ningpo. M.: Xaohing. C'est le Sciaohino de Ricci, distinct du Sciaochino (=Shiuhing) au Kwangtung. Ecrit: Ciquione (…), Ciaushino, **Sciauhim**, et « Xaohim simile nella voce a Xaokim, residenza del Viceré di Cantone » (St V, p. 707); Schauhsing »*. The vow formula's « Chao-him » belongs to this attested spelling family (-him/-hing contamination was known to Ricci himself), and Dehergne explicitly fences it off from the Kwangtung homophone Shiuhing/Tchao-k'ing (Zhaoqing).
- **Dehergne 1957, place index, printed p. 133:** *« Shaohing, Chao-hing — Che 18 »*, on a separate line from *« Shiuhing, Tchao-k'ing — Kwtg 13 »* — the author's own disambiguation.
- **Dehergne, *Répertoire* (1973), printed p. 357**, in the note to the "Carte des résidences de Chine en l'année 1701": *« La Congrégation de la Propagande ne possède alors aucune résidence, **celle de Siaoshan du Shaohing au Chekiang** n'ayant été achetée que le 9 nov. 1702 »* — Shaohing used as the *fou* containing Siaoshan (蕭山), i.e. a prefecture-level entity; the Propaganda (MEP) presence there in 1702–1706 is the immediate mission context of Contancin's French-mission Chekiang years.

### 4. VERDICT — 🟢 confident: Shaoxing 紹興 (绍兴), Zhejiang

- The candidate is right. Dehergne himself normalises the vow formula to Shaohing; his 1957 Chekiang entry gives the characters **紹興 *fou*** and lists precisely the "-him" spellings the register uses; Pfister prints **紹興府** for the same romanisation in the same province. The homophone risk (Shiuhing/Tchao-k'ing = Zhaoqing, Guangdong — which Ricci noted sounds alike) is excluded by the formula's own « prov. Tche kiam » = Tchö-kiang/Chekiang *(inference, but Dehergne's: it is his basis for "=Shaohing")*, and by Contancin's documented Chekiang stay from 1707 (Dehergne printed p. 59; Pfister printed p. 572).
- **Wikidata anchors** (read 2026-10-14): the event happened at the seat of the Qing prefecture 紹興府 = **Q17006595** (P31 府 Q839328 + former territorial entity Q19953632; P131 Q28411084 浙江省(清朝) 1645–1911; P571 1131; seat coordinate 30.0045, 120.5783). The candidate **Q42666 = 绍兴市**, the modern prefecture-level city (P31 Q748149; P131 Q16967 Zhejiang; P442 `33 06`; coordinate 30.0, 120.5833). The two are the same urban seat — **0.7 km apart**; **Q42666 is the id to field** if the project anchors the modern city (its usual practice), with Q17006595 the historically exact alternative. Geography check against the author's own words « entre Hangchow et Ningpo » (1957, printed p. 20): Hangzhou→Shaoxing 50 km ESE, Shaoxing→Ningbo 95 km E — matches.
- Caveats (labelled): (i) ARSI *Gallia* 16, f. 108 not consulted — the identification rests on Dehergne's quotation and normalisation, which is about as strong as printed evidence gets; (ii) the 1957 note documents the Ming-period christianity; the characters and the *fou* identity carry over to the Qing prefecture of the same name and seat; (iii) Wikidata quirks on Q17006595: its zh-hans description wrongly says "今江苏省境內" (should be Zhejiang, as its en/zh descriptions say), and it lacks P576/P1366/P150; a possible duplicate exists (Q113651402 紹興府, no description). None of these affect the identification.
- For the sources (flag only — no `.cli` edit made here): `ls$jesuita-votos-local/Shaohing/17060114` (dehergne-c.cli, line 1946) lacks `@wikidata:` — **Q42666** should be added; and the transcription's obs carries "(Gai. 16, 108)", a misreading of **Gal.** 16, 108.

### 5. What would settle it

Consulting **ARSI, *Gallia* 16, f. 108** (the profession formula of 14 Jan 1706 — it may name the church/house, e.g. the Jesuit house at Shaohing vs a dependent residence), and the contemporary mission catalogues (JS 134 ff.) for Contancin's station in 1705-1706. Nothing in the printed record points anywhere but Shaoxing; only the unconsulted register could refine *where in the prefecture* the vow was made.
