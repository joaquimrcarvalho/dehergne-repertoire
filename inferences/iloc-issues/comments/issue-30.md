## 1. What the printed notice says

**DEHERGNE, *Répertoire des Jésuites de Chine de 1542 à 1800* (1973), notice no. 659 — « Posateri, Antonio (ital.) Mgr. » — printed p. 209:**

> «… N. « Siculus Panormitanus », Palerme, 30 avril 1640 … E. 22 oct. 1656, Palerme. Emb. le 2 avril 1674 pour le Brésil (W 1154) et arr. 4 nov. 1676 … Kwangtung; 1684, Shanghai, Nankin (Kiangnan); **1691, Chen-si; Kan-sou; 1696, premier vicaire apostolique du Chan-si** (SOCP 1696, 374); 1700, Song-kiang … P. avant 1675; **élu vic. apost. du Chan-si, 20 oct. 1696** (SF V, 383). … M. 18 janv. 1705, T'ai-yüan fou, Chan-si (non encore sacré). … Pf. 387. MARGIOTTI, 792.»

**That is the whole of the geographical record.** The name-column is *Tchang Ngan-tang, Tsing-Tchai* (Dehergne's romanisation of the Chinese name); the alternative spellings are *Posatery*, *Pusateri* (*JS* 163, 163), *Possateri*. No station, residence, church or county appears anywhere in the notice, and the printed forms are **Chen-si**, **Kan-sou**, **Chan-si** — there is no "Chang-si" place-name in the volume.

## 2. The identification, and its limits

- **`Chang-si` = Chan-si = 山西 Shanxi**, the Chinese province, and the centre of gravity of the last decade of Posateri's life: *premier vicaire apostolique du Chan-si* from 1696, *élu vic. apost. du Chan-si, 20 oct. 1696*, dead at **T'ai-yüan fou (太原府), Shanxi** in 1705. The QID candidate **Q46913 (山西省 Shanxi)** is the right *kind* of item — a province — and should be recorded as such.
- **No finer identification is possible from the printed sources.** Dehergne gives provinces only; Pfister (see §3) indexes the same three provinces; the archive holds no Chinese gazetteer, no Shanxi mission catalogue, and not Margiotti's *Il cattolicismo nello Shansi* (Rome, 1958), which Dehergne cites ("MARGIOTTI, 792") and which is the obvious place to look for a station-level answer. **The honest outcome is a province-level anchor; a village or county QID would be invented.**
- **1691 is a visitation, not a residence.** The three provinces in the record — Chan-si, Chen-si, Kan-sou — are the northern districts; the printed itinerary puts **Shaanxi and Gansu** in 1691 and **Shanxi** from 1696. If the 1691 row is kept, the provinces to record for that year are **陝西 Shaanxi (Q47974)** and **甘肅 Gansu (Q42392)**; Shanxi belongs to the 1696 vicariate, not to the 1691 journey as printed.

## 3. Pfister's notice 145 — what can and cannot be quoted here

Pfister's own index gives the notice exactly:

> **PFISTER, *Notices*, t. 2 (1934), *Table alphabétique*, printed p. 1077:** « POSATERE **145, pp. 387, 388**. — … Koang-tons, 388; — Kiang-nan, 388, 410; — **Chan-si, 388, 591**; — **Chen-si, Kan-sou, [465]** … »

and the same volume's index of places, **printed p. 1000**, reads « **Kan-sou 甘肅 : le P. Posateri, 388** » — corroborating the two provinces of the 1691 clause. (The index form "Posatere" is the OCR's split of *POSATERI* from the Chinese name in the same line, 党**tang**/Tang.)

**A limitation the reader should know:** in the Pfister t. 2 copy consulted, **notice 145 itself is not present** — the digitised excerpt runs from printed p. 921 (notice 427, Étienne Yang) straight to printed p. 923 (notice 428, Aloys Kao), and printed pp. 387–388 are not in it. **No sentence of Pfister's notice is quoted in this comment**; the page references come from Pfister's own index and from Dehergne's "*Pf. 387*" (Dehergne states in his abbreviations, printed p. XXXV, that "*Pf.*" = page, not notice number). A complete copy should be consulted before anything from notice 145 is used verbatim.

**Backer–Sommervogel** (*Bibliothèque de la Compagnie de Jésus*, 12 vols., 1890–1909) has **no entry or index entry** for Posateri / Pusateri / Possateri / Posatery (all twelve volumes searched).

## 4. The two record corrections

**(a) "1716, Pékin" is not Posateri.** The printed passage it comes from is DEHERGNE, notice **no. 824 — « de Tartre, Pierre Vincent (Du Tartre) (franç.) P. » — printed p. 264**:

> «… 1704, Taiyüan, Shansi; 1707, le légat de Tournon lui accorde les pouvoirs pour le Shansi, 1707-1715 (Margiotti, 81); Pékin; 1710, Leao-tong; **1711, provicaire de Mgr Posateri au Shansi; 1716, Pékin (JS 177, 58)**; où le 18 févr. 1718, supérieur de la résidence française…»

Both clauses describe **de Tartre** ("provicaire **de** Mgr Posateri au Shansi" = de Tartre was *Posateri's* vicar in Shanxi), and Posateri had died on **18 January 1705**, eleven years earlier. The row should be moved to **de Tartre (Dehergne no. 824)** and worded "1711, provicaire de Mgr Posateri au Shansi; 1716, Pékin". The province named is, again, **Shansi = 山西**.

**(b) "1691, Chan-si" should carry the province of the vicariate, not of the journey.** Recommended wording: *"1691, Chen-si (Shaanxi) et Kan-sou (Gansu) — visitation; 1696, premier vicaire apostolique du Chan-si (Shanxi)"*, with **Q46913 (Shanxi)** attached as the province of office and the 1691 provinces as separate, coarser anchors if the data model allows.
