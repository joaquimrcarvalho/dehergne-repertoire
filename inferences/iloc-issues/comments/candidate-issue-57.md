## Archive research report (2026-10-14)

Person: **João Duarte** (`deh-joao-duarte`, signs *Joannes Eduardus*; Dehergne 1973 no. 266; Pfister notice 247; Chinese name per Pfister: 聶若望 *Nié Jo-wang*). Event under study: `jesuita-votos-local`, profession ("pr." → fielded `4V`), 1706-08-15.

### 1. Itinerary (compact)

N. Lisbon 1671-11-27 (JS 134, 399v) · E. 1690-07-17 (or 1689, JS 134, 435) · emb. 1696 (W 1361) · A. 1700-04-22 (JS 134, 399v) · **1703 Hunan** (JS 199 I, 116v) · **1704 Siangtan, Hunan** · **vows "Sian tan hien" 1706-08-15 (*Lus.* 13, 19)** · 1725 Hukwang (JS 134, 422a) · vice-provincial of China 1728-1729 · 1740 Hukwang · provincial of Japan 1741-1746 · M. Macao 1752-01-06 (HS 53a, 75). Pfister adds: first stay at Siang-t'an 湘潭縣 **1700-1703**, church-building at Heng-shan 衡山縣 1702, new churches at Heng-tcheou 衡州 and Yong-tcheou 永州 to the south, "2 résidences, 12 églises et 45 oratoires" to the north (Welt-Bott n° 87); back at Siang-tan through the Yung-cheng persecution, still there 1738-39.

### 2. The attestations, with printed pages

- **Dehergne, *Répertoire des Jésuites de Chine* (1973), entry 266, printed p. 80** (the whole notice is on this page): *« 1703, Hunan (JS 199 I, 116v); **1704, Siangtan au Hunan**; 1725, Hukwang (JS 134, 422a); … **V. 15 août 1706, Sian tan hien, pr. (Lus. 13, 19)**. Il signe Joannes EDUARDUS. »* — the same notice that carries the vow formula also places him at **Siangtan** (Hunan) in 1704, two years before the vows. Primary reference behind the vow: **ARSI, *Lusitania* 13, f. 19** (not consulted). The entry also cites *Pf. 558*.
- **Pfister, *Notices*, t. 1, notice 247 (LE P. JEAN DUARTE 聶若望), printed pp. 558–559:** *« Il quitta Lisbonne, sa patrie, en 1696, et fut missionnaire assez longtemps à **Siang-t'an 湘潭縣 (Hou-nan)**. Il y fit un premier séjour de 1700 à 1703. »*; and (same page): *« Le préfet de la ville de Heng-chan 衡山縣, chrétien pieux, fit construire à ses frais une église … (1702). Plusieurs nouvelles églises furent alors fondées à Heng-tcheou 衡州 et à Yong-tcheou 永州 au sud de Heng-chan, tandis qu'au nord il y avait 2 résidences, 12 églises et 45 oratoires (Welt-Bott, n° 87, 31). »*; *« Il était de retour à Siang-tan au moment de la persécution de Yong-tcheng … Il y était encore en 1738 et 39. »* — **Pfister prints the characters 湘潭縣 for Duarte's own mission station**. Note his header date *« P. 15 août 1705 »* vs Dehergne's 1706 (see §4).
- **Dehergne, *Répertoire* (1973), "Deuxième partie", list of the 1701 residences, printed p. 358**, under « HUKWANG (actuel HUNAN + HUBEI) »: *« Changsha R'; et **Siangtan R** »* — Siangtan recorded as a residence with resident missionary(s) exactly during Duarte's first stay (Pfister: 1700–1703). (The 1701 residence map itself, an unnumbered plate at the end of the volume, marks Siangtan too; the list on printed p. 358 gives the name in type.)
- **Dehergne, « Les chrétientés de Chine de la période Ming (1581–1650) », *Monumenta Serica* 16 (1957)**: no attestation of Siangtan — the article covers the Ming christianities 1581–1650, and Hunan is outside its scope. No other consulted work adds a different spelling.

### 4. VERDICT — 🟢 confident: Xiangtan 湘潭, Hunan — field **Q215755**

- The candidate is confirmed as to the *place*: **the author's own works give both the place and the characters for this very man**. Pfister, whom Dehergne cites ("Pf. 558"), prints **湘潭縣** for Duarte's mission station "Siang-t'an (Hou-nan)"; Dehergne's own notice spells the same place "Siangtan au Hunan" (1704) and renders the vow register's formula as "Sian tan hien".
- **Phonetics resolved** (this was the flagged risk): in Dehergne's French-based romanisation final *-ng* is nasalised/lost before a consonant, so *Sian* = *Siang* = 湘 *xiāng* — and here we do not need the inference, because the same notice prints "Siangtan" and Pfister prints 湘潭縣. *Tan* = 潭 *tán*; *hien* = 縣 *xiàn* "county" (Wade-Giles *hsien*), which also explains why the fielded form has the extra syllable the 1704 line lacks: the register named the **county**.
- **Mission geography excludes alternatives**: Duarte's documented world is the Xiang river corridor of Hunan — Siangtan 1700-1703 and 1704, Hengshan/Hengzhou/Yongzhou 1702 (all given with characters by Pfister), Hukwang 1725/1740. Xiangtan lies ~61 km at 7° (N by NNE) of Hengshan county — Pfister's "au nord" side, where he counts "2 résidences, 12 églises, 45 oratoires". No competing "Sian/Siang-tan" exists in his district or anywhere in Huguang mission geography.
- **Wikidata — the fielded id should be Q215755, not the issue's Q1375015.** **Q215755 = 湘潭市 Xiangtan**, the prefecture-level city in Hunan (P31 Q748149/Q1549591; P131 Q45761 Hunan; P442 `43 03`; P625 27.843, 112.923). It is the id this repository already uses for the 1704 "Siangtan" stay in the same man's entry, and the historic 湘潭縣 seat (on the Siang-kiang, the mission station) lies inside today's Xiangtan City. **Q1375015 = 湘潭县 Xiangtan County** (P31 county Q1289426; P131 Q215755 — two-way verified; P442 `43 03 21`; P625 27.7826, 112.9455) is the *residual modern county*, whose seat moved to Yisuhe 易俗河 in the 1950s and which is 7.1 km at 342° from the historic seat; it is a **county-level alternative only**, not the historic station. There is no separate item for the Qing-era 湘潭縣. The issue's other candidate **Q703818 is rejected**: it is **Yongjia County 永嘉縣, Wenzhou, Zhejiang** (P442 `33 03 24`), not in Hunan and not connected with Duarte.
- **Date discrepancy (flag only)**: Pfister's header has *« P. 15 août 1705 »*; Dehergne, citing the register (*Lus.* 13, 19), has 15 août **1706** — Dehergne is the later, archival-based work and the project's 1706-08-15 should stand. Likewise Pfister's "M. après 1750" is bettered by Dehergne's M. 6 Jan 1752, Macao (HS 53a, 75).
- **For the sources (flag only — no `.cli` edit made)**: `sources/dehergne-d.cli` line 932 `ls$jesuita-votos-local/Sian tan hien/17060815` lacks `@wikidata:` — **Q215755** should be added; optionally normalise the value's spelling to "Siangtan" per Dehergne's own 1704 line and Pfister.

### 5. What would settle it

Nothing in the printed record leaves doubt about the identification itself; only finer detail could be added by **ARSI, *Lusitania* 13, f. 19** (the profession entry — it may name the church or the residence within the county) and the Hunan catalogues **JS 199 I** for 1703-1706 (Duarte's station at the vow date), plus *Welt-Bott* n° 87 for the 1700-1703 first stay. If the project ever distinguishes the Qing county seat from the modern county, a historical item for 湘潭縣 (seat = today's Xiangtan city core) would be the exactly correct anchor.
