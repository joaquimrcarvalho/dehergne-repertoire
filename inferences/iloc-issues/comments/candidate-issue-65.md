## Identification report (14 October 2026)

### 1. The person's itinerary (Dehergne 1973, no. 575, printed p. 183)

Manuel da Motta (modern Mota; he signs "Emmanuel de Motta"; not to be confused with Manuel da Matta), Portuguese Jesuit priest, no. 575:

- b. 20 July 1720, Braga (Lus. 48, 149); "creât. Mag. Art." (JS 134, 442v); entered the Society 19 May 1736 (Lus. 48, 214).
- Embarked as a non-priest for China (W 1941) in 1748 — his name is struck from the catalogue Lus. 48, 214 and 361 for 1748. Pfister (notice 388, printed p. 835): reached Macao in 1749, in company of Fr. Toussaint Masson; "Il ne put, toutefois, pénétrer dans les provinces" before 1754; still alive "au Hou-koang en 1777" (letter of P. André Rodrigues, 15 Aug 1777, in de Murr, Journal, t. VII, p. 266).
- 1754, China central; 1757, Hou-nan = **Hunan** (SOCP t. 55, 1765, 355 — Dehergne writes "Hunan (Hou-nan)" for the same province in entry no. 54).
- 1765: 82 Christian communities scattered over the **Hou-kouang** (Huguang) mission, mostly small ones (SOCP 55, 1765, 355).
- **Vows: "V. « Emanuel da MOTTA, U cham fu, Hu Kuam », 13 déc. 1767, pr. (Hisp. 34, 476)"** — 4th vows (pr.), signed from U cham fu, Hu Kuam. Primary reference as cited, not consulted (ARSI, Hispania 34, f. 476).
- d. 1782, in the Hou-nan (Hunan).

So at the vow date da Motta was a missionary of the Hukwang/Hunan district, and the vow formula places him at "U cham fu" in "Hu Kuam" (Huguang).

### 2. Findings

All quotations are given from the printed works; page numbers are as printed.

1. **The entry itself** — Dehergne 1973, no. 575, **printed p. 183**: the vow line reads verbatim *« V. « Emanuel da MOTTA, U cham fu, Hu Kuam », 13 déc. 1767, pr. (Hisp. 34, 476) »*. The `.cli` field (`U cham fu, Hou-kouang`) reproduces it faithfully, with Dehergne's own province spelling normalised.
2. **Dehergne's own identification of the very string** — entry no. 54 (Agostinho de Avellar, Portuguese, of the same Hunan/Hukwang district), **printed p. 18**: *"missionnaire au Hunan (Hou-nan) en 1761 (SOCP 55, f° 77 et 355; à « U Cham fu » **(Wuchang)** en 1767 (Hisp. 34 II, 476)"*. Same source folio (Hisp. 34 II, 476), same year 1767: Dehergne glosses «U Cham fu» = **Wuchang** himself. This is the author's cross-identification, not our reconstruction.
3. **Dehergne 1957** (*Les Chrétientés de Chine de la période Ming*, Monumenta Serica 16), place note **Hupeh 4, n. 8, printed pp. 49–50**: *"8. WUCHANG (Ou-tch'ang) **武昌府** fou, capitale du Hukwang (et du Hupeh). M. : Vuchang, le seul nom du Hou-quang surmonté de l'indication «IHS» (=résidence jésuite). Ecrit: **Vucham fou** (Ba. IV 1062); Vucianfu (…); **Utschang-fu** (VII 3267). Il y a un Wuchang hien dudit fou, plus à l'est."* And, on the residence: *"En 1637, le P. Ant. de Gouvea … vient fonder la résidence de **Hucham fu** dans le **Hu quam** (G. 445…; Ba. IV 1062)"*. This documents the Portuguese-era spellings of the Jesuit residence — Vucham fou / Hucham fu / Utschang-fu, "dans le Hu quam" — exactly parallel to the 1767 signature "U cham fu, Hu Kuam" (Portuguese orthography: w- written u-/v-, chang written cham).
4. **Pfister, Notices, t. 2** — da Motta's own notice no. 388, **printed p. 835**, gives no place beyond Hou-koang; but the Laimbeckhoven station list, **printed p. 761**: *"à Ou-tch'ang fou **武昌府** 4 WF, 1739, 1740, 1743; à Té-ngan fou 德安府 …"* — Wuchang fu as the mission's station in exactly this period; and the Addenda, **p. 41***: *"p. 760, note 3, ajouter: Ni 1736, ni 1737: le P. de Laimbeck-hoven écrit de Ou-tch'ang, le 14 oct. 1740, que le 2 février de cette année (1740), il fit à Han-k'eou les quatre vœux solennels de religion entre les mains du P. des Roberts…"* — the Wuchang/Hankou stretch of the Yangtze hosted 4th-vow professions one generation before da Motta (cf. issue #50's dossier: Laimbeckhoven at Wuchang fu 1739–43; the Baiquan 柏泉 vow station in the same Han-river corridor).
5. **Dehergne 1973 appendix**, list of Ming residences, **printed p. 354**, under HUKWANG: *"WUCHANG 1637 / Tungshan 1629 / …"* — Wuchang is the residence of the Hukwang mission from 1637 on.
6. Further Dehergne 1973 usages, all equated with 武昌府: no. 116 (Britto), **printed p. 37**: "évêque de Wuchang (Wou-tch'ang fou), diocèse à créer, 9 oct. 1711"; nos. 573–574 (Jacques and Nicolas Motel), **printed pp. 182–183**: "1663, Wuchang fou, Hou-kouang (JS 134, 345)"; "M. 2 août 1692, Wuchang (Wou-tch'ang fou) (HS 50, 38: «in Vù Consù» (sic)…)".

### 4. VERDICT: 🟢 confident — 武昌府 Wuchang fu, Wikidata **Q11124318** (the reviewer's Q1112431 is wrong)

- The place of Manuel da Motta's 4th vows of 13 Dec 1767 is **Wuchang fu 武昌府**, the capital of Huguang/of Hubei — today **Wuchang District, Wuhan, Hubei** (Q1014420).
- **The author himself says so**: Dehergne 1973 glosses the identical string «U Cham fu» as "(Wuchang)" in entry no. 54 (printed p. 18), with the identical archival reference (Hisp. 34 II, 476) and the same year 1767 — Avellar and da Motta, two missionaries of the same district, are both recorded at U cham fu in 1767 by the same register.
- **Phonetics and characters are documented, not guessed**: Dehergne 1957 (printed pp. 49–50) gives the characters 武昌府 and lists the Portuguese-era spellings of this Jesuit residence — Vucham fou, Vucianfu, Utschang-fu, Hucham fu "dans le Hu quam" — which map letter for letter onto "U cham fu, Hu Kuam" (w- → u-/v-, chang → cham, Hu-kuang → Hu quam/Kuam).
- **Context fits**: Wuchang was the principal Jesuit residence of the Hukwang mission (1637 foundation, Dehergne 1957 n. 8 and appendix p. 354; station 1739–43 in Pfister p. 761), i.e. the natural house at which a scattered Huguang district missionary (82 chrétientés in 1765) would make his solemn vows; Laimbeckhoven's 1740 vows in the same Wuchang–Hankou corridor (Pfister, Addenda p. 41*) show the pattern.
- **Wikidata (read 2026-10-14)**: **Q1112431 is an NGC galaxy (NGC 4546)** — the reviewer's code is a mis-typing. The correct item is **Q11124318 武昌府 "Wuchang Fu"** (P31 = Q839328 府/fu prefecture; P131 = Ming Huguang Q17031144, Huguang Q85882093, Qing Hubei Q28411551; P571 1364; P576 1912; P1365 replaces Yuan Wuchang Lu Q11124347; P625 30.5564, 114.3106 = the prefecture seat). Modern anchor: **Q1014420 武昌区**, district of Wuhan (Q11746), Hubei (Q46862), P442 `42 01 06`; Q1014420's own P131 includes Q11124318, tying the modern district to the historical fu. For the `.cli`, `@wikidata:Q11124318` is the right anchor for the 1767 vow place, with Q1014420 as its modern successor if a modern location is wanted.
- **A second "Wuchang Fu" item to avoid**: **Q28414894** also carries the English label "Wuchang Fu", but its Chinese label is **五常府** — a prefecture in Manchuria, unrelated to Huguang. Use only **Q11124318**.
- **Homophone caution, resolved**: Dehergne 1957 notes "Il y a un Wuchang hien dudit fou, plus à l'est" — a distinct 武昌县 of the same prefecture (modern Ezhou 鄂州). The vow signature says **fu**, and the residence glossed by Dehergne is the prefecture capital, so the county homonym is excluded.

### 5. What would settle the remainder

- The fu-level identification needs nothing further. What remains open is only the *building*: consulting **ARSI, Hispania 34 (II), f. 476** (the profession record itself, cited by Dehergne, not consulted here) could show whether the formula adds a church/residence detail, and who received the vows; **SOCP 55 (1765), f. 355** would map da Motta's 82 communities and confirm his district's ties to the Wuchang house.
