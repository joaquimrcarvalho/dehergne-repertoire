## Identification report (14 October 2026)

### 1. Itinerary of Étienne Faber (Dehergne 1973, no. 279, printed p. 85)

Born 14 Feb 1597 at Mourières near Avignon; entered the Society at Avignon 26 Sept 1615; ordained priest 1627 (Avignon); embarked 3 Apr 1629 (W 752); Macao Oct 1630; 1632 Kiangchow, Shansi (under Vagnoni); Shensi from c. 1635 — founds the chrétienté of Hanchung (Han-tchong) c. 1635; profession ("4V") 31 May 1640 at Si-ngan-fu (Lus. 6, 5); Sian 1640 and 1646; Beijing with Schall 1641–1644; back in Chen-si 1647 with da Costa; Hanchung (Han-tchong) fou 1646; **died 10 May 1657 at "Siao-tchai du Han-tchong", Shensi** (date per Rouleau; HS 48, 127v gives 4 Aug; Pfister, printed p. 202, still gives 22 May 1659 at Han-tchong).

### 2. Findings

- **Dehergne 1973, entry no. 279, printed p. 85**: *«M. 10 mai 1657 (comme le prouve ROULEAU; et non 4 août, comme le dit HS 48, 127v), à « Siao-tchai du Han-tchong », Shensi.»* Note: the printed entry itself writes the itinerary line "Hanchung (Han-tchong) fou, **Shansi**" — a misprint in the book (Hanzhong is in Shensi); the `.cli` copies it faithfully.
- **Dehergne 1973, 2e partie, II. Chronologie, printed p. 359**, under SHENSI: *«Hanchung R'; **Siao-tchai du Chengku** R'»* — Dehergne himself locates the Siao-tchai residence **in Chenggu (城固) county**, i.e. in the Hanchung (= Hanzhong) prefecture, not in the prefectural city itself.
- **Dehergne 1957** (*Les Chrétientés de Chine de la période Ming*, Monumenta Serica 16), Shensi §2 Chengku, **printed pp. 110–111**: *«Il y a aussi une chrétienté à "Siao tchai tse" **小寨子** "Siao tsai pe" près de Chengku. Vers 1635, le [pays] est délivré par deux fois des sauterelles, grâce aux prières du P. Le Fèvre, qui a une florissante chrétienté à Siao tchai tse; de même, en 1641, il délivre le mont "Ts'inling" 秦嶺 entre Sian et Yanghsien, des tigres qui l'infestent (Pf. 203, 205).»*  Place index **printed p. 133**: *«Siao-tchai-tse — Shensi 2»* (= the Chengku subsection).
- **Pfister, Notices, t. 1, notice 65 (P. Étienne Faber ou Le Fèvre), printed pp. 202–206**: **printed p. 206**: *«Son tombeau se voit encore près de **Siao-tchai-tse 小寨子**, chrétienté dont il fut le fondateur… le fleuve **Han-kiang 漢江**, qui coule près de là, ayant changé son cours… les bourgs ou villages de Siao-tchai-tse 小寨子, Fong-kia-ing 馮家營, Yu-kia-ing 余家營, et Wang-p'ou-tse»* (letter of Mgr Chiais, 25 Apr 1873) —  So the village lay **on the Han river**, next to villages called 冯家营 / 余家营 (the -营/-堡 micro-toponomy of the Han valley plain in Chenggu).
- **Pfister, Notices, t. 1, notice 107 (P. Claude Motel), printed p. 300**: *«il avait aussi l'administration de **Han-tchong, de Tch'eng-kou, de Siao-tchai** et de plusieurs autres endroits… Ils avaient construit à **Siao-tchai** une belle et vaste église»* (after Greslon and Gabiani) — Siao-tchai is listed as a distinct station alongside the Hanzhong city and Chenggu county.
- **Modern Chinese sources** (web, read 2026-10-14): Faber's tomb (方爷坟) stands at **小寨村, 董家营镇 (formerly 乡), 城固县, 汉中市**, on the south bank of the Han river 汉江 at the confluence with the Nansha river 南沙河 — exactly Pfister's "près de… Han-kiang"; the church at 城固小寨 is called the first Catholic church of southern Shaanxi (陕南第一座天主教堂). Local veneration of 方德望 as a protector god still attested.

### 4. VERDICT: 🟢 confident — place confirmed, Wikidata code must be corrected

**The place is 小寨(子) Xiaozhai, in Chenggu county (城固县) of the Hanzhong prefecture (汉中府), Shensi = modern 小寨村, 董家营镇, 城固县, 汉中市, 陕西省.**

Reasoning:
1. **Phonetics**: Siao-tchai(-tse) = xiǎozhài(-zi) 小寨子 exact match, including the 子 suffix that Dehergne 1973 drops; both Pfister (p. 206) and Dehergne 1957 (p. 110) print the characters 小寨子.
2. **The author's own identification**: Dehergne 1973 himself writes "Siao-tchai **du Chengku**" (printed p. 359) and Dehergne 1957 files Siao-tchai-tse under his Shensi §2 = Chengku county, "près de Chengku" (printed pp. 110–111, index p. 133). The "du Han-tchong" of entry 279 refers to the Hanchung (= Hanzhong) prefecture/mission, not to the city.
3. **Topography**: Pfister's notice (Han river flowing past the village, neighbouring villages 冯家营/余家营) and the modern tomb location (south bank of the Han at the Nansha confluence, 城固县董家营镇小寨村) coincide.
4. **Wikidata chain (read 2026-10-14)**: the **documented** anchor is the county — **Q1069968 城固县 (Chenggu County)**, P442 `61 07 22`, P131 → **Q515573 汉中市**. The modern village **小寨村 Q13763099** lies inside **Q13763015 董家营镇** (P442 `61 07 22 119`), itself a child of Q1069968; Q13763099's P131 is Q13763015 and its P442 is `61 07 22 119 214`. The historical 小寨子 has **no Wikidata item**; the equation "historical 小寨子 = modern 小寨村, 董家营镇" rests on name + county + position and is an **inference**, so link the county (Q1069968), or, if the modern-village inference is accepted, the town Q13763015 / village Q13763099. The village item has **no coordinate**; the nearest coordinate carrier is the town 董家营镇 Q13763015 (33.0762, 107.2666) = 22.5 km due east (88°) of the old Hanchung-fu seat (Hanzhong city centre) and 10.2 km SSW (212°) of the Chenggu county seat — consistent with "près de Chengku".
5. **Rejected alternatives**: **Q515573 is NOT 小寨 — it is the prefecture-level city 汉中市 (Hanzhong) itself** (P31 prefecture-level city, P442 `61 07`, coord. 33.079/107.026). The contributed candidate line "小寨 (Xiaozhai), Q515573" conflates the village label with the prefecture item; linking the death to Q515573 would only assert "died in Hanzhong". Also rejected: 小寨 in Xi'an (Q107327016/Q10958733) and 小寨镇 Lantian (Q10958742) — that is Sian, a different station; 小寨站 in 勉县 (Q10958727, railway era, wrong county); the other 小寨/小寨子村 of Hanzhong prefecture (武乡镇 Q13757549, 新街子镇 Q13774215, 阳平关镇 Q13775932, 硖口驿镇 Q13777810, 渔渡镇 Q13778677) — all in the wrong counties.
