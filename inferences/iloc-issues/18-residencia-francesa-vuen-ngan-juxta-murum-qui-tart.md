## Identification

**Conclusion.** **Identified (the name), with one unresolved qualification.**
Dehergne himself, in his gazetteer of the Peking mission, equates the 1717
residence *« Vuen ngan juxta murum qui Tartaros a Sinis dividit »* with
**Wenan (Wen-ngan) hien 文安** — a county of **Shuntian prefecture (順天府)**,
*south of Peking*, today **文安县 Wen'an County, Langfang, Hebei** →
**[Q1196389](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1196389)** (the modern county, an
inference from Dehergne's identification, not an archive statement). The page
is Dehergne 1953, entry no. 42 (printed p. 330): *"42. Wenan (Wen-ngan) hien
文安, sud de Pékin, est écrit Vengan par NIEUHOFF et Voen-gan-hien par
D'ANVILLE. … En 1717, les Jésuites français occupent la résidence de Vuen ngan
« iuxta murum qui Tartaros a Sinis dividit » (Catal. 1717)."* The phonetic fit is
exact in Dehergne's system (*Vuen-* = 文, *-ngan* = 安; 萬安 would be *Wan-ngan*,
and Dehergne's 1957 gazetteer treats a *Wanan* 萬安 village of Kaoyang, Baoding,
as a different place).

**Qualification.** The Latin tag cannot be squared with a county in the plain
some 100 km south of Peking, and **no page in this archive explains it**; the
only archive source for the Peking residence is Dehergne (quoting ARSI *JS* 134,
f. 451), since **Pfister's notice of Prémare never mentions the place** and keeps
him in Kiangsi throughout. The Great-Wall readings proposed in the issue (宣化
Xuanhua, 萬全 Wanquan, 延慶 Yanqing) have **no support on any page of this
archive**. Full research note: `notes/vuen-ngan-premare-identification.md`.

**On the "juxta murum" qualification.** The TGAZ point is **118.9 km** from the
顺天府 seat (39.93143, 116.368), i.e. well out on the plain south of Peking —
consistent with Dehergne's "sud de Pékin", and *not* next to the Great Wall
(which lies north of Peking). The other `文安` hits are unrelated (a Han-era
文安郡 and 文安驿 in Shaanxi); TGAZ offers no wall-side 文安 **homonym** that
would rescue the Latin tag. The qualification therefore remains exactly as the
issue states it: the name identification is 文安, the *« iuxta murum »* phrase
is unexplained by any gazetteer evidence.

**Verdict: `confirmed`.** TGAZ independently records 文安县 as a county under
顺天府 at the Dehergne date, 0.8 km from the Wikidata anchor `Q1196389`. It
confirms the issue's *name* identification and its administrative frame; it
says nothing in favour of the *« juxta murum »* tag, which stands unresolved.
