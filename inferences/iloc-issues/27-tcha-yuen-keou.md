## Identification

**Conclusion.** **Identified** as **茶园沟 (Cháyuángōu)**, the valley in the
western mountains of Gucheng county (谷城县), Hubei — the Jesuit station the
French sources call the *montagnes de Kou-tch'eng* / *« Cévennes de la Chine »*,
whose church stood on **磨盘山 Mopanshan**. Administratively it is today
**沈垭村, 紫金镇, 谷城县, 襄阳市, 湖北省** (Shenya village, Zijin town, Gucheng
county, Xiangyang, Hubei) →
**[Q14091289](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q14091289)** (沈垭村). The characters
are an inference from the romanisation (Dehergne writes the **unaspirated**
`-keou`, whereas 口 *kǒu* would be `k'eou` as in his own `Han-k'eou` 漢口, so the
final syllable is **沟**), read together with the modern Chinese toponymy; no
source in the archive gives the characters. Maur Ts'ao made his vows there on
26 March 1759, five days before his colleague Mathurin Lamathe (1 April 1759).
Full research notes: `notes/chayuangou-identification.md` and
`notes/chayuangou.md`.
