## Identification

**Conclusion.** Not identified at village level — **no Wikidata id should be
added**. The archive documents only the prefecture: **Linkiang fou = 臨江府**,
seat **清江縣 (Tsingkiang)**, modern **樟樹市 / 臨江鎮 (Zhangshu, Yichun,
Jiangxi)**. Dehergne's notice 911 (printed p. 301) gives the arrest as
*« arrêté à Pé-pou du Linkiang fou, mai 1787 »* with **no Chinese characters**,
and its single source is the **Archives des Missions Étrangères de Paris**
(Dehergne's siglum `M.`), cited as `M 447, 1385` — **not in this archive**. Greps over
doc 65, the doc 73 gazetteer and place index, and docs 74/75 produce neither the
name nor its characters; the phonetic candidates 白埠/北埠/百步 cannot be chosen
without invention. A prefecture-level anchor only (`Q197394` Zhangshu, or the
historical `Q28414898` Linjiang Fu) would be defensible; a village-level one is
not. Full research note: `notes/pe-pou-linkiang-identification.md`.
