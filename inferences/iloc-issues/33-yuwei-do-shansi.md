## Identification

**Conclusion.** Identified (as an explicitly labelled inference) as **右衛
(Youwei)**, the old Ming frontier garrison in northern Shanxi = the Qing
**右玉縣 (Youyu County, 朔州市, Shanxi)** → **`Q1196894`**. In 1725 the Chinese
Jesuit Rosario Ho (Dehergne's *Ho T'ien-Tchang K'i-Wen*) was deported from Canton
into exile there, and Dehergne's notice 722 (printed p. 231) has him at "Yuwei du
Shansi" in
1727 — the same year Pfister says he made excursions "jusqu'aux frontières de
Tartarie pour y visiter les princes exilés par Yong-tcheng" (printed p. 410). The
UZH *China and the West* database, built on Dehergne, adopts the same reading:
"He Tianzhang … wird ins Exil nach **Youwei (Youyu, Shanxi)** geschickt" (1725);
"1727 … in **Youyu (Shanxi)** tätig". Dehergne gives **no Chinese characters**,
and the passage he quotes — BnF **4-O2N-441** = *Literæ sinenses…* (1731), **p. 16** —
is not in this archive,
so the reading rests on the romanisation, the exile context and that external
database. Do **not** use `Q56533878` ("右卫"), which is the imperial *guard
institution*, not the place. Full research note:
`notes/yuwei-shansi-identification.md`.
