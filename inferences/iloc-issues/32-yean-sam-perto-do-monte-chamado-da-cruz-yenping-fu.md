## Identification

**Conclusion.** **Not identified at the locality level; only the Yenping anchor is justified.** Every independent witness in the archive puts Aleni's death in **延平府 Yenping, today 南平 Nanping, Fujian** — Dehergne's Ming gazetteer is explicit: *« 31. YENPING (Yen-p'ing) **延平** fou, au centre de la province, actuel Nanping **南平** … Aleni, réfugié en 1647 meurt là le 3 août 1649 »* (doc 73, §31, **p. 40**, characters read from the page image), and Pfister: *« M. 3 août 1649, Yen-p'ing. »* (t. 1, no. 30, **p. 126**). The form **"Yen-sam" occurs only in Dehergne's own notice 17**, quoted from a manuscript this archive does **not** hold — ARSI *Japonica-Sinica* **49-V-13, fol. 483v** — and nothing in the archive locates the **"mont appelé de la Croix"** (十字山) either. The archive's own date (3 Aug. 1649) also differs from the notice's 10 June 1649. Full research note: `notes/yean-sam-aleni-identification.md`.

Source fix: `sources/dehergne-a.cli:421` — keep Dehergne's quotation but drop the appearance of a village-level anchor and add `@wikidata:Q1337959` (延平區 Yanping District, the district containing the old 延平府 seat; already used for *Yenping* at `sources/dehergne-locations-1644.cli:291`), e.g. `ls$morte/Yean-sam, Yenping, Yen-p'ing, Fukien (延平府, hoje Nanping, Fujian)#a fonte diz «para o monte qua chamao da Cruz» (十字山); lugar de aldeia não identificado/@wikidata:Q1337959/16490610`. Alternative: `Q68534` (南平市 Nanping, prefecture-level city).
