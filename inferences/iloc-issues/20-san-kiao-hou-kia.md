## Identification

**Conclusion.** **Identified** as **三橋 (San-kiao / Sanqiao)**, a market town
**15 li (≈ 7–8 km) south-east of the seat of 瑞州府 Ruizhou fu**, Jiangxi —
present-day **三桥村, 荷岭镇, 高安市 (Sanqiao village, Heling town, Gao'an City),
Yichun, Jiangxi** → **[Q14583988](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q14583988)**
(proposed modern anchor). Dehergne himself brackets the place as *"près de
Juichow (Jouei-tcheou), Kiangsi"*, and his 1957 gazetteer gives the characters:
*"JUICHOW (Joei-tcheou) 瑞州府 fou, actuel Kaoan 高安 … Un certain 胡應茂 Hou
In-meou émigre de San-kiao 三橋 (bourg à 15 li sud-est de Juichow)"*. The fuller
vow formula *San-kiao hou-kia* reads **三橋胡家** ("Sanqiao of the Hu family") —
an **inference** (Dehergne gives no characters for *hou kia*), which fits the
Hu-family community described in the 1957 notice. The same chapel — the chapel
of the Blessed Virgin Mary at San-kiao — served both Le Febvre's profession
(19 March 1741) and Pierre Tch'en's vows (8 September 1745). Full research note:
`notes/san-kiao-ruizhou-identification.md`.

Note on spelling: the printed Dehergne reads ***San Kiao*** (橋 = *kiao*), in
both no. 459 and no. 830; a form *San Kia* is a variant reading, not what the
volume prints. "San-kiao" here is the place **三橋**, not the
Buddhist-Confucian phrase 三教 *san-kiao* ("the three religions").
