## Identification

**Conclusion.** **Identified** as **通州 Tōngzhōu of Zhili / Hopei** — the
Grand-Canal port about four leagues east of Peking, today **通州區 Tongzhou
District, Beijing** → **[Q393836](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q393836)**. It
is **not** the 通州 of Jiangsu (today 南通 Nantong), which Dehergne's parenthesis
rejects, nor the homophonous 同州 of Shaanxi, nor the fourth namesake in
Kweichow. The identification is Dehergne's own, and the archive carries it
twice: his 1957 gazetteer entry no. 15 (*"Tungchow (T'ong-tcheou) 通州, port
fluvial à 4 lieues de Peking, à l'est. Actuel Tunghsien 通縣. … Distinct du
Tungchow … du Kiangsu; du Tungchow fou du Shensi; d'un Tungchow du Kweichow"*)
and the 1953 Peking gazetteer entry no. 40 (*"Tungchow … D'ANVILLE: Tom-tcheou,
port fluvial à l'est de Pékin; actuel Tunghsien … il faut assurément lire « Tum
cheu »"*). Full research note:
`notes/tum-cheu-tongzhou-identification.md`.

**The homonym trap.** The bare `通州` query returns **22** records spanning
several provinces and eras (a Tang-era 通州 in Sichuan at 31.23/107.50, the
Jiangsu 通州 at 32.01/120.85, the Zhili one, and a 通州關/通州里 village pair in
Kweichow). The **year filter at 1767 returns only the Jiangsu homonym** —
`hvd_33310` 通州 (直隶州, 1724–1911, parent 江苏省) and `hvd_91118` (1724–1767,
no parent) at 32.01047/120.85464 (today's 南通 Nantong) — because the Zhili
record is in the 1820 layer only. So a year-filtered query alone would have
produced exactly the wrong place. `南通` itself returns **0 hits**: TGAZ knows
the Jiangsu unit only as 通州.

**Best match.** `hvd_103` — 通州 (Tong Zhou), department (州), CHGIS **1820**
layer, parent **順天府** (Shuntian Fu); 39.90592 N, 116.65881 E;
`present_location` "北京通县". Canonical record:
<https://chgis.hudci.org/tgaz/placename/json/hvd_103>. A second Zhili record,
`hvd_196673` 通州 (本州, 1911, parent 順天府, no coordinates), is the 1911
continuation of the same unit.

**Coordinates.** TGAZ 39.90592, 116.65881 vs Wikidata `Q393836` (通州区 /
Tongzhou District, Beijing) 39.90858, 116.65133 → **0.7 km**. The Jiangsu
homonym `hvd_33310` is **955 km** away.

**Verdict: `confirmed`.** TGAZ has the Zhili/Hebei 通州 under 順天府 with
coordinates 0.7 km from the Wikidata anchor `Q393836`; the year filter's Jiangsu
result is the homonym Dehergne's parenthesis rejects. The 1820/1911 records
postdate the 1767 vow but record the same unit (通州 remained a 州 under
順天府); no change to the issue's conclusion.
