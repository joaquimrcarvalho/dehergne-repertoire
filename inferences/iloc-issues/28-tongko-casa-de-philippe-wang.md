## Identification

**Conclusion.** **Not identified.** Both elements of the name occur **once** in
this archive, in Dehergne's notice no. 401 (Henriques, António-José), printed
p. 124: *« arrêté le 21 déc. 1747 à Tongko près de "Tsangzo" dans la maison de
Philippe WANG (entre Soochow et Kashing (Kia-hing fou) »* — quoted from the
**trial dossier of the Archives des Missions Étrangères de Paris** (Dehergne's
siglum `M.`, vol. 444, ff. 339–356 and 427–456), which is **not held here**.
Pfister's long notice of Henriques (no. 322, printed pp. 705–710) narrates the
same arrest **without naming either place**: it places the capture on
**21 Dec. 1747** at the house of the **family Song 宋**, on Henriques's flight
from Sou-tcheou toward Kia-hing, while the earlier arrest of P. de Athemis
(11 Dec. 1747) was at **Philippe Wang's** door — so the two sources also differ
over the house. Dehergne's 1957 gazetteer and its *Table des lieux* (printed
pp. 132–135) contain **no entry for either name**. The readings proposed in the
issue (**東港 / 同港 / 塘口**) are phonetically possible but rest on no
documentary attestation, and **no Wikidata id is justified**. Full research
note: `notes/tongko-identification.md`.
