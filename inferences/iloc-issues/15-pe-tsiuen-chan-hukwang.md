## Identification

**Conclusion.** Identified as **柏泉山 / 柏泉 (Baiquan shan / Baiquan)**, near
**茅廟集 (Mao-miao-tsi)** in Hukwang (Hubei), the locality roughly 30 km from
Hankow glossed by Dehergne — the modern **柏泉, 東西湖區, 武漢市, 湖北省**
(Baiquan, Dongxihu District, Wuhan, Hubei). The decisive archive page is
**Pfister t. 2, printed p. 745, n. 1**: « Probablement **柏泉**, près
Mao-miao-tsi **茅廟集** » (with Pfister's index at printed p. 1070:
« «Pé-tsiuen-chan» (**柏泉山**?) (Hou-koang) »); Dehergne himself glosses the
same place "à 30 Kms de Hankow". External corroboration: the KU Leuven
CCT-Database renders Desrobert's 1741 letter as written at
« Huguang sheng, **Boquanshan 湖廣省柏泉山** ». The candidate `Q144663` in the
file is **wrong** (it is 衡陽市 Hengyang, Hunan). Full research note:
`notes/pe-tsiuen-chan-identification.md`.
