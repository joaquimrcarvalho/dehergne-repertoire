## Identification

**Conclusion.** Identified as **小寨子 (Xiao zhai zi)** — the "gros bourg" and
Christian community founded by Étienne Faber (Le Fèvre) about 1635 **near 城固縣
(Chenggu hien), in 漢中府 (Hanzhong fou), Shensi** = modern **城固縣, 漢中市,
陝西省**. The characters are in the archive: Dehergne 1957 gives « Siao tchai tse
» **小寨子** (printed pp. 110–111) and his place index files it as "Siao-tchai-tse
**Shensi 2**", Shensi item 2 being the Chengku 城固 entry (printed p. 133);
Pfister prints « Siao-tsai-pé (Siao-tchai-tse **小寨子**) » among Le Fèvre's new
Christians of 洋縣, 城固縣 and 漢中府 (printed p. 203). The historical village has
**no Wikidata item**; anchor at the county **`Q1069968` (城固縣)** — or
`Q13763015` (董家營鎮) if the modern-village reading (inferred) is accepted. Full
research note: `notes/siao-tchai-hantchong-identification.md`.
